# Agent instructions

## Mission and scope

This is a research-first repository. Deliver an auditable metastudy, critical synthesis, general dossier, applied research paper and evidence-to-design foundation. Do not turn the research phase into a production TTS implementation or write a conclusion before the evidence exists.

## Start every task

Read [RAG.md](RAG.md), [the programme](docs/PROGRAMME.md), [the runbook](docs/AGENT_RUNBOOK.md), the complete target issue and all comments, then the issue's topic authorities and actual upstream deliverables. Reconcile live `main`, branches, PRs, checks and dependency acceptance. A closed issue or merged placeholder alone is not evidence of completion. Prior chat, search snippets and seed references are discovery context, not authoritative findings.

## Authority and amendments

Follow platform/security requirements and the user's explicit current instructions first. Within this repo, these instructions and the runbook govern execution; the frozen research protocol and recorded amendments govern method; issue scope governs the work package; provenance-backed records govern factual claims. RAG is an index, not a competing evidence source. Conflicts require a documented reconciliation, not silent reinterpretation or weakening acceptance.

## One mutable repository

Only `techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics` is mutable in this task. Other repositories/folders are read-only. Use issue-scoped branches and disjoint files for parallel workers; one integrator owns shared manifests, generated aggregates and merges. No force push to `main`, destructive cleanup of another worker's branch, stale local human-queue authority, or changes to repository protections/credentials. A local human-queue staging note is not canonical; see [human gates](docs/HUMAN_GATES.md).

## Evidence integrity

Never invent citations, readings, quotes, metadata, participant data, statistics, reviewers, permissions, affiliations or endorsements. Record access, reading extent, verification agent and independence separately. Use primary sources for decisive claims and exact page/section/table/paragraph or edition locators. Inspect source images where tables/figures or extraction errors require it. Distinguish observation, author claim, interpretation, hypothesis and project preference. Do not imply Brier endorsed emotion-free machine speech. Do not assume arbitrary acoustic codes have universal meanings or no affective consequences.

Multiple model passes may catch errors; they do not constitute independent human screening, peer review, ethics approval or empirical validation. Structural CI cannot establish scientific truth. Keep synthetic fixtures separate from live research and propagate source corrections downstream.

## Work through to landing

For authorized issue scope, carry out the work, tests, documentation, PR, review fixes, exact-final-head checks, squash merge and post-merge verification. Do not stop merely at a draft, pushed branch or opened PR. Do not bypass required approval/checks or claim an absent CI run passed. Partial/gated PRs use `Refs #N`, not closing keywords. Close the issue only when all its acceptance criteria are met. See the runbook for exact completion evidence and blocked-state handling.

## Working style

Default to high reasoning effort; deeper effort is appropriate for critical synthesis or statistical review, not routine bookkeeping. Subagents are permitted for disjoint source batches, read-only critique and independent calculation cross-checks; disclose their actual role. Use concise, heading-rich, hyperlink-navigable Markdown. Documents with six or more numbered headings need a contents list with one-line summaries. Avoid ornamental terminology and unsupported certainty. Use end-to-end execution language in prompts; do not use the user's previously rejected autonomy adverb.

## Real human gates only

Prepare materials and continue unblocked scope. Escalate only genuinely necessary lawful access, permissions, authorship/license decisions, external reviewer action, participant/ethics arrangements, or required repository approval. No routine project-management burden should be shifted to the user. Never contact, submit, purchase, recruit or expose participants without the relevant authorization. Maintain a precise checkpoint rather than fabricated completion.
