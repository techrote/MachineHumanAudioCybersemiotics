## Resume rule

A previous status summary is a pointer, not authority. Re-fetch the target issue and actual artifacts. Do not repeat already completed work or adopt a stale checkout's instructions. The [runbook](docs/AGENT_RUNBOOK.md) defines the required checkpoint and completion record.
