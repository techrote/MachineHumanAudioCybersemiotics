# Empirical validation preparation

[Foundations](PROJECT_FOUNDATIONS.md) · [Human gates](HUMAN_GATES.md) · [Integrity](RESEARCH_INTEGRITY.md)

## Boundary

MHAC-R014 prepares protocols and synthetic analysis rehearsal. MHAC-R020 is the separately authorized real-participant extension. No participants, real listening exposure, recruitment, external registration or device experiments are authorized by the bootstrap. Simulation tests analysis logic and planning assumptions, not human meaning, usability or effectiveness.

## Staged questions

First establish construct and stimulus validity: can listeners distinguish urgency from severity, uncertainty from low confidence in the interface, source identity, acknowledgement and resolution? Check whether a supposedly non-human voice is actually perceived that way while preserving intelligibility.

Next compare communication components without confounding message structure, cue family, voice rendering, acoustic duration/level, lexical content and training dose. Separate first exposure, trained performance and delayed retention. Conventional speech and speech-derived cues remain serious comparators. A product constraint does not justify a biased comparison.

Finally test realistic task conditions: divided attention, background speech/noise, multiple sources, repeated alerts, interrupted/superseded messages, incorrect machine estimates, acknowledgement without resolution and requests for clarification. Use safe simulated workshop/desktop contexts initially, not dangerous physical failures.

## Design and analysis contract

For each experiment specify a narrow hypothesis, competing explanation, estimand, primary and secondary outcomes, manipulation checks, task/materials, recruitment population and language/hearing boundaries, randomization/counterbalancing, masking where feasible, exclusions and deviations, missingness, multiplicity, analysis model and revision criterion. Freeze confirmatory decisions before relevant data exposure. Label exploratory pilot decisions honestly.

Primary outcomes should include correct interpretation and appropriate action, alongside errors and omissions. Latency must not ignore failures or selectively report only successful responses without an explicit estimand. Secondary outcomes may include training time, retention, repeat requests, workload, annoyance, source confusion, perceived human likeness and attributed emotion. Distinguish listener affect from emotion apparently expressed by the machine.

Choose sample-size or precision planning from a smallest meaningful effect and justified variance/dependence assumptions; use sensitivity ranges where pilot estimates do not exist. Do not invent preliminary data or pick a round sample size and back-justify it. Rehearse analysis with clearly marked synthetic data, stable seeds and known-answer tests. Keep it physically and logically separate from real evidence records.

## Ethics, access and privacy readiness

Prepare consent, participant information, withdrawal and data-handling procedures; appropriate ethics/oversight arrangements; recruitment authorization; safe listening/calibration and stop procedures; accessible alternatives; and the scope of data collection. Record the actual approving authority or justified oversight arrangement, not a fabricated committee. Verify applicable requirements when preparing the real study rather than copying a generic legal claim.

No personal participant or hearing/health records belong in this public repository. Plan de-identified derived releases only where consent, rights and risk assessment permit. Audio recording or identifiable voice storage needs its own explicit justification and permission; it is not required merely to test comprehension.

## Failure and revision criteria

Predefine what would make a cue or framework unsuitable: consequential misunderstanding, poor retention, excessive training, source confusion, high interruption cost, an ineffective human-likeness manipulation or a result explained entirely by simpler message wording. A shorter sound is not necessarily a better communication system. Negative results revise the design; do not relabel them as success.

## Required outputs

A hypothesis-to-study matrix, preregistration-ready protocol, synthetic rehearsal pipeline and tests, source-backed methodological rationale, sample-planning sensitivity analysis, reproducible materials specification and exact human-readiness checklist. External registration is optional and permission-dependent; never claim it occurred because a protocol was committed. Actual participant findings remain out of scope until the separate gate is satisfied.
