## Two complementary written outputs

The extended dossier preserves the general investigation of Brier and related work: intellectual development, concepts, arguments, ontological commitments, comparisons, criticisms and unresolved questions. It should remain valuable without the audio project. Organize it into navigable chapters with exact source locators and a critical reading guide, not an unstructured literature dump.

The main paper is one coherent integrative review-and-framework article. Working title: **From Signal to Situated Understanding: Cybersemiotics and the Design of Explicit Machine–Human Audio Communication**. Its contribution must emerge from the evidence and subtraction test, not a prewritten claim of novelty or efficacy.

Write the abstract after the results and argument exist. Distinguish research findings, author claims, synthesis interpretation, design hypotheses and future work throughout. Do not let the final discussion claim more than the actual methods permit.

## Scholarly quality gates

Verify novelty against existing auditory-alert reviews and semiotic engineering. Give competing accounts substantive treatment. Audit every load-bearing claim and numerical result; source locators and correct attribution are mandatory. Tables/figures must regenerate from committed records. Treat a broad ontology and a successful lab task as different kinds of evidence. Include unresolved disagreements when reconciliation would distort the sources.

No word-count target substitutes for depth. The dossier can hold extended scholarship; the article should be sized for a coherent contribution and, later, a selected venue's actual requirements. Do not invent a venue fit, author affiliation, DOI, reviewer endorsement or acceptance. Journal selection, external submission, author consent and licensing require their own authorized decisions.

## Reproducible package

Deliver Markdown sources, verified bibliography, protocol/search/screening/identity/extraction/appraisal/claim records, methodological amendments, analysis code and version/seed information, generated tables/figures, design traceability, experiment protocols, reviewer/audit records and a rights/privacy-aware manifest with hashes. Keep restricted full texts and private data outside the public bundle.

Document a clean-checkout reproduction path. If PDF/DOCX or other rendered reading outputs are generated, follow available format-specific tooling instructions and inspect their visual output, citations, tables and pagination. Do not add a fragile publication toolchain before the manuscript stage needs it.

## Completion states

`planned → research_draft → internally_audited_package` describes the core. `externally_critiqued`, `submitted`, `peer_reviewed/accepted` and `participant_evaluated` are separate evidence-bearing statuses, not automatic successors of a merge. MHAC-R018 may finish the internal package while #19/#20 remain gated. A source-limited package must identify its scope and cannot silently waive unsatisfied core acceptance.

The final implementation handoff should be short and traceable: established project constraints, supported findings with conditions, promising hypotheses, arbitrary conventions, known failure modes and the next discriminating research tasks. Avoid embedding provisional conclusions as permanent product instructions.
