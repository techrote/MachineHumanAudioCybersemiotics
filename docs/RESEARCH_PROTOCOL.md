**Status: instructions for constructing the protocol, not a frozen or preregistered protocol.** MHAC-R002 pilots and freezes the executable version under `research/protocol/`. Record actual dates; the proposed initial literature cut-off is 2026-10-04. Prior exposure to Brier, audio cues and the project's preferred direction must be disclosed.

## Purpose and dual tracks

General: reconstruct and critically evaluate Brier's programme, its intellectual dependencies, development, explanatory contribution, commitments and limitations independently of this application.

Applied: investigate which parts improve the specification, evaluation or design of machine–human audio communication. Neither Brier's correctness nor the superiority of non-anthropomorphic audio is a predetermined result.

Working success criterion: adequate situated understanding and appropriate action/coordination, with total interpretive effort including training, interruption, repetition and repair. Message duration alone is not the outcome. This criterion is our proposed research framing, subject to conceptual refinement.

## Research questions

| RQ3 | What is known about comprehension, action, learning, retention and workload for speech and non-speech audio? | Systematic evidence map; conditional quantitative synthesis. |

| RQ6 | What design contracts and evaluation criteria follow, and what would cause revision? | Traceable requirements and empirical protocols. |

## Methodological architecture

Use a systematic empirical evidence-map strand and a critical interpretive/theoretical strand with separate selection rules. Apply statistical meta-analysis only to compatible data. Do not label a purposively sampled theoretical corpus an exhaustive systematic census.

The empirical strand freezes eligibility, outcomes/estimands, search plans and analysis decision rules after transparent pilots and before production selection/extraction. The theoretical strand uses versioned sensitizing concepts, purposive sampling, contrary cases and explicit amendments as concepts develop. Freezing every theoretical definition in advance would defeat the interpretive aim. Any theoretical revision that changes empirical eligibility must trigger an impact assessment and documented rescreening, not silent cherry-picking.

Methodological anchors to inspect directly are [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020), [PRISMA-S](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z), and [Dixon-Woods et al.'s critical interpretive synthesis paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2288-6-35). The first two are reporting resources, not quality certificates. The CIS adaptation must explain its differences from the original healthcare application. Evaluate additional evidence-map, non-pooling and AI-review reporting guidance in MHAC-R002; do not assert compliance without an item-by-item map.

## Pilot and freeze contract

Pilot diverse source types and counterexamples; test query recall against known relevant seeds without optimizing solely for them. Calibrate eligibility, identity resolution and extraction fields. Record actual reviewer/agent roles, disagreements and unresolved cases. Select realistic search channels and explain inaccessible ones. Document novelty relative to existing systematic audio-alert reviews and semiotic engineering.

Freeze v1 by commit/hash and date with the pilot/exposure statement, exact queries, eligibility, record schemas, outcome families, statistical decision rules, appraisal and verification plans, stopping rules and access limitations. A Git commit provides a versioned internal record; it is not an external preregistration or an independent timestamp/registration service. External registration needs separate authorization and truthful status.

## Eligibility and scope decisions

Define populations, tasks, acoustic representations, comparisons, outcomes and study designs for each empirical subquestion. Include relevant null/negative results and comparators outside the intended product style. Record language/translation limits, grey literature, historical records and task/domain boundaries. Do not require empirical papers to cite Brier. Do not use statistical significance, open access or preferred cue design as eligibility criteria.

Core theory selection follows actual argument dependencies: primary Brier texts, required Peircean/cybernetic/system/pragmatic sources, related HCI theories and substantive criticisms. Claims about whole books need appropriate reading coverage; selected chapters support bounded claims only. Retain unavailable reports as an access category, not as irrelevant exclusions.

General TTS benchmark races, unrelated neuroscience and universal consciousness debates are out of scope unless essential to a specific argument. No real-world safety efficacy is established by transferring results from a laboratory, aviation or medical domain.

## Appraisal and verification

Assess theory by fidelity, coherent premises/inference, explanatory scope and objections. Assess empirical work by design validity, measurement/construct validity, dependence, selective reporting, uncertainty and transfer conditions. Do not force one aggregate score onto both.

Plan human verification realistically. AI extraction is provisional with source locators and auditability. Repeated models are correlated tools, not independent humans. Report the lack of dual human screening or specialist review where applicable; it limits the claims that may be made. A source-fidelity audit must cover every load-bearing quotation, result and inference used in the final manuscript.

## Completion and amendments

Empirical search completion requires all protocol channels executed or explicit access-gap/coverage limitations, plus complete recorded screening and reconciliation. Theoretical sampling ends under a reasoned saturation/negative-case log and a bounded scope statement, not a paper-count target. Resource limits justify transparent amendments or scope-limited claims, not undisclosed omissions.

Each amendment records old/new rule, reason, author/agent, date, prior evidence exposure, affected records/claims and required rework. Final search refresh is mandatory before internal release. A sparse, inconclusive or entirely non-pooled evidence base is an acceptable honest result; empty records cannot be called a completed review.
