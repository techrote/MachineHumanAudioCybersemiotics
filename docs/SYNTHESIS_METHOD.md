# Critical and empirical synthesis method

[Protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) · [Data model](DATA_MODEL.md) · [Paper plan](PAPER_PLAN.md)

## Keep distinct kinds of contribution distinct

Theoretical reconstruction explains an author's argument faithfully. Critical comparison assesses assumptions, scope and alternatives. An evidence map describes the distribution and limits of empirical work. Meta-analysis estimates compatible quantitative effects. An engineering model specifies a proposed system. None automatically validates the others.

Our synthesis must expose the connections and breaks between these activities. A philosophical commitment is not an experimental measurement; a successful cue-recognition task does not confirm a broad ontology; an elegant state schema does not prove improved human comprehension.

## Brier reconstruction

Read primary text and record definitions, premises, reasoning, explanatory targets and the development across versions. Examine the roles of semiotics, second-order cybernetics, observer/context, embodiment, autopoiesis and subjective/intersubjective perspectives using the sources themselves. Keep source claims, interpretation and proposed application separate. Do not reduce the general inquiry to interface slogans.

Identify commitments essential to Brier's account versus those merely useful as engineering inspiration. Record internal tensions and strong alternative readings. Missing core text means a bounded reconstruction, not permission to infer an entire book from reviews.

## Comparative and interpretive synthesis

Use a problem/construct/assumption/mechanism/prediction-or-payoff/objection matrix. Compare relevant alternatives, including pragmatics/common ground/repair, semiotic engineering, grounding and formal or cognitive accounts. Preserve incompatible uses of meaning, information and agency. Distinguish equivalence from analogy. Critique the construction of the research problem itself and the project's aesthetic preferences.

The method is adapted from [Dixon-Woods et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2288-6-35), whose interpretive approach permits iterative conceptual development and theoretical sampling. Document that adaptation; it is not a warrant to revise empirical eligibility after seeing desirable results. Use versioned analytic memos and purposive negative-case searches. Explain stopping and residual uncertainty.

## The remove-Brier test

Remove the cybersemiotic terminology and reconstruct the design using conventional HCI/human-factors accounts. Identify what changes: a question, omitted variable, feedback requirement, interpretation of evidence, prediction, design decision or organization of knowledge. If nothing substantive changes, report that applied contribution as limited or redundant. If the contribution is conceptual organization rather than prediction, say so. Do not protect the name at the expense of the result.

For every integrative proposition record its strongest counterexample, alternative explanation, boundary conditions and what evidence would require revision. Research can inform the project even if it rejects a preferred acoustic design or does not substantiate a unique cybersemiotic contribution.

## Empirical map

Group by actual task/domain, cue family and semantic content, population/hearing/language, training dose, repeated exposure/retention, acoustic conditions and outcome. Do not pool all studies called earcons or speech as if those labels define homogeneous treatments. Compare source uncertainty and risk of bias within relevant outcome families. Keep preference, perceived urgency, correctness, errors, workload and response latency distinct.

Map existing reviews and primary studies without double-counting samples. Record inaccessible or incomplete reports and assess how they limit coverage. Results from specialized safety settings retain their original conditions and do not certify a general desktop/workshop interface.

## Conditional quantitative analysis

Specify estimands, eligible comparisons, dependence handling and missing-data assumptions in the protocol before outcome-driven selection. Use verified standard methods implemented in versioned tools with tests against known examples; do not invent a sophisticated-looking estimator for a small corpus. Record effect direction, units, dispersion, sample size, repeated-measures correlation assumptions, shared controls and overlapping samples.

Decide pooling separately for each defensible comparison. Incompatible constructs are not repaired by a random-effects model. Address uncertainty, heterogeneity, effect multiplicity and sensitivity to appraisal/overlap/missingness. Apply publication-bias or moderator analyses only when the corpus supports them. Justify choices and report unidentifiable quantities rather than overfit them.

Where pooling is unjustified, show structured estimates/directions and uncertainty with design and construct differences. Do not count statistically significant studies as votes. Distinguish inconclusive evidence from no effect. A wholly non-pooled synthesis may complete #10 if transparently justified. No universal ranking of cue families is required.

## Original analysis and tests

Propositions must identify variables, predicted relation, plausible mechanism and revision criteria. Example candidate, not a finding: separately representing time-to-action and consequence severity may reduce confusion between urgent low-hazard events and less-immediate high-hazard events. Test competing explanations and the cost of training, retention, repetition and repair.

Examine intelligibility separately from human likeness, urgency separately from attributed emotion, and listener affect separately from apparent machine emotion. Acoustic mappings may be simultaneously learned, structurally suggestive and state-coupled; classify the relevant relation instead of assigning a sound a permanent semiotic category.

## Audit and downstream propagation

Every decisive statement has source locators or an explicit inferential chain. Audit all load-bearing citations and quantitative entries; specify any sampling used on lower-impact material. Preserve disagreements. Final source/search updates trigger re-analysis and changes to claim, design, dossier and paper records as needed. CI checks reproducibility and integrity, not theoretical correctness or independent human judgment.
