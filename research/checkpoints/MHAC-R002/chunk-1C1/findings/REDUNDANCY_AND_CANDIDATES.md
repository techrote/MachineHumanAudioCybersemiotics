## Strongest justified redundancy objection

The existing review explicitly names auditory icons, earcons, spearcons and speech, and declares systematic review/meta-analysis of brief human–machine alerts in its title. MHAC RQ3 proposes speech/non-speech evidence mapping and conditional synthesis. **If MHAC merely produces another broad cue-family comparison and repackages the resulting recommendations, the empirical contribution risks being a duplicate or update of this existing review.** A new cybersemiotic vocabulary, a preference for non-human sound, an added traceability schema, or the fact that MHAC uses PRISMA reporting guidance does not by itself answer that objection.

This objection is deliberately conditional. The comparator's actual outcomes, cut-off, primary corpus, estimands and recommendations have not been read; claiming specific duplicate results or even identical eligibility would exceed the evidence. A later temporal update could be useful, but its actual start boundary cannot be set from the publication year. An updated review is not automatically conceptually novel, and novelty is not required for a transparently scoped replication/update to have value.

## Candidate question: understanding versus appropriate action

**Basis:** 1A RQ3; 1B P06 and the adaptation memo's outcome/codebook distinction; comparator title entries. The question is whether recognition, message comprehension and correct requested action require separate syntheses or evaluation criteria once task, training and retention are explicit. This is an inherited project distinction, not a newly discovered review gap.

**Contrary reading:** Nees–Liebman or the underlying studies may already make these distinctions; MHAC could merely rename them. **Required test:** read the comparator's actual outcome definitions, characteristics tables, training/exposure and limitations before selecting any narrower empirical extension. Any effect claim then needs the relevant primary reports. If existing work already answers the question, label the remaining work as reuse, replication or updating, not an original contribution.

## Candidate question: contextual message semantics beyond existing HCI

**Basis:** 1A RQ2/RQ4/RQ6; 1B P09/C06/C07 and its confidence/urgency distinction; comparator's title-declared scope only. **Contrary reading:** established human-factors or semiotic-engineering accounts may already provide the same distinctions. **Required test:** inspect this comparator's definitions and design discussion, then the relevant verified edition/sections of de Souza's *The Semiotic Engineering of Human-Computer Interaction*. Identify what changes under the remove-Brier test; no extra label or data field suffices. Performance benefits require separate empirical evidence, not conceptual elegance.
