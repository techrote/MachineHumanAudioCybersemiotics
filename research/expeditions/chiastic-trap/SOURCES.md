## What the computation actually contributes

The mathematical claims are *not* derived from a reading of a rhetorical or musicological authority. They follow from a fully specified toy model. They are constrained by their definitions, open to counterexample, and machine-exhaustible over three states. Human listeners, rhetorical intention and cultural convention are not represented as variables in the model.

The source-derived observation and the new formal model answer **different questions**:

- **Dubremetz & Nivre:** can candidate lexical crossings be detected with useful precision in parliamentary text under particular annotation and model conditions?
- **X006:** does a mirrored sequence of abstract event labels determine whether a state-transition composite is identity?

A superficial ABBA detector fails as a rhetorical classifier in many contexts; an **intentional** ABBA sonic score still cannot certify its event action. These are logically distinct negative arguments that reinforce a boundary but are not the same empirical observation.

## Three rival interpretations to preserve

**Rival A: The decorative-only view.** The four-note audio is merely ornamental; no listener expects it to communicate a state. X006 cannot exclude this. If accepted, it deflates the practical risk while leaving the finite counterexample intact.

**Rival B: The conventional-code view.** A trained community could legitimately agree that ABBA means "ready for verification" or another arbitrary token-level message. Such conventional meaning is possible but must be specified; it still would not prove that a purported operational return actually occurred. In this account, the problem is *evidentiary*, not a universal semantic prohibition against using symmetry.

**Rival C: The perceptual-asymmetry view.** Even an identical list of motif tokens may fail to be heard as a mirror because envelopes, pitch contrast, masking, hearing, tempo or context change salience. This remains undecided. Literal sample-level reversal is deliberately not claimed by the renderer.

## Disconfirmation and correction protocol

A reviewer could invalidate the **mathematical core** by supplying one state in the declared finite permutations where the executable witness and hand-derived composite disagree. The unit tests enumerate all states and several negative controls.

A reviewer could invalidate the **synthetic identity claim** by finding any differing WAV byte between the two case outputs from the same generation run. Both hashes and a direct comparison are recorded.

A reviewer could narrow the **rhetorical account** by correcting a source's definition, reading the original *Ad Herennium* passage or finding a better historical taxonomy. Such a correction would change the humanities commentary, not automatically the finite algebra.

A future **human study** could challenge the hypothesised perceptual hazard in either direction; this expedition records **no** human evidence. No source check or AI review is a substitute for independent academic evaluation.

## Discovery-only questions

- Is there a historical rhetorical figure for *the appearance of reciprocity without actual reciprocity* that fits better than the present chiastic analogy? Do not invent an ancient technical term.
- Do twentieth-century musical analyses of retrograde distinguish heard recovery from notated symmetry? Obtain lawful primary score/source passages rather than relying on circulating anecdotes.
- Would a non-palindromic yet state-closing pattern be aesthetically less satisfying to listeners trained on a code? This is an untested comparison.
- Could a deliberate mismatch between an elegant sonic cadence and independently visualized non-closure improve an educational instrument? This is an artistic proposal.
