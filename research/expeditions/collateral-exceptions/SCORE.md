# A Brief Permission to Disagree
Original score for four voices and an optional boundary player, November 2026.

Use four distinctive instruments or four distinguishable electronic voices A, B, C, D. Give each two clearly distinguishable registers 0 and 1. For example, each voice has a separate base pitch (A3, C4, E4, G4) and switches between plain and gently modulated timbre. The notes need not become the same when the labels are equal. A label is a stipulated rule state, not a claim about perceived acoustic similarity. A fifth performer may play a quiet distinct tick for each violated equality edge. All sound optional; cards and marked lines suffice.

## I — A line

Write AB, BC, CD on three cards. These are the only equality predicates. Sound 0000, then 1111, at any comfortable dynamic. Return to 0000. Select AB as the intended exception. Move directly to 0111 simultaneously, holding the principal voices for a few breaths. The boundary player sounds only AB. Do not count the number of individual label flips as the number of violations. Return to an agreed register or wait.

## II — A loop

Add DA while all four voices are at 0000. Do not announce a new movement through sound. Perform the same 0111 gesture. Boundary player sounds AB and DA, in that order. Try to realize AB alone, without changing the rules. The performers may discover the impossibility by asking what the other three equalities would entail, or simply leave the attempted gesture unfinished. Do not improvise a successful solution to an inconsistent instruction.

## III — The republic of redundant laws

Add AC and BD; all six unordered pairs are now present. Play 0000, 1111, 0000 and 0111 as before. The principal performance is unchanged. Sound AB, AC, AD in the boundary part. Try 0101, whose boundary consists of AB, AD, BC, CD, and compare to 0111. Neither is declared superior aesthetically. You may choose the less economical departure.

## IV — The exception that refuses its optimization

Restore 0000. Remove all but AB, BC, CD. Demonstrate that both unanimous register assignments remain legal. Choose a new target edge. The director may request the smallest boundary, but the players can reject that optimization, substitute a different legal rule graph, or end. The boundary player may keep their cards visible while remaining silent.

An alternative coda: keep all six cards on a table without performing them. Let someone remove one of the cards. No sound is required. The score concerns relationships, not a general duty to represent them acoustically.

## Fictional programme note — the municipal bridge

The town had four clocks and a law for each pair. Whenever the clocks agreed, the clerk added another law to protect the agreement. The clockmaker eventually asked to be allowed one disagreement. The clerk issued a permit naming a single pair.

The clockmaker returned with three forms. The clerk objected that two had not been requested. The clockmaker laid the six laws on the table. She proposed keeping three, and demonstrated that not one permitted arrangement of perfectly synchronized clocks would be lost.

The clerk asked whether she was proposing a better clock. She said that she was proposing a different exception.

That winter the town commissioned a concert. The clock faces did not move. A percussionist played the laws that would have to be crossed if one clock were to differ.
