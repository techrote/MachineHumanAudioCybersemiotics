# Return Address — original text score

For a room, one player, and a second person or independently authored sound source. All participants must agree to the intervention. A performance may use synthetic stems instead of live people or environmental recording. No prescribed audience exposure, recording or level. Do not suppress warnings or necessary accessibility sounds.

## I. Borrowing

Begin by noticing an existing sound without deciding whether it belongs to the work. The player may introduce a quiet sound of their own, chosen in relation to that existing sound. They are not required to imitate it. Let the new sound develop slowly enough that it could be mistaken for an ordinary constituent of the space. In a synthetic rehearsal use the in-phase or disjoint witness.

After a chosen interval, remove only the introduced layer. Do not bow at the moment of removal. Let the other source determine its own duration.

If there is no existing sound the player wishes to use, the movement may be unperformed.

## II. A loan with negative interest

Perform using synthetic stems only. Choose an existing tone and an opposite-polarity duplicate. Introduce the duplicate gradually. Do not apply cancellation to an actual room or anyone else's sound.

At the point of digital cancellation, remove the duplicate. The original tone returns to the mix. The player is to mark this event on the score as a subtraction, even though total mean-square energy increases.

Repeat with an equal-power tone at a disjoint frequency. Compare the ledger of the player's contribution with the ledger of the resulting mix. Neither ledger is a report of what somebody heard.

## III. The unclaimed orchestra

Two performers each prepare a small layered patch. They may nominate parts of their own sound to withdraw. They cannot instruct the other to stop. Any remaining voices may continue after the nominal end.

A third, optional performer holds a blank page. They may decline to declare any surrounding sound part of the work. No microphone is necessary. There is no requirement to produce quiet. If a person needs to leave or speak, the score imposes no obligation upon them.

## IV. Return address

A final layer is removed. The performer does not replace it. The end of the layer is not the end of the surrounding activity.

There is no applause instruction.

## Fiction — The Municipal Lease of Audible Air

At the end of the first century of the city's speaking machinery, the planning office discovered that it had been renting everybody the same room.

The tram company leased the lower octave. The weather bureau leased a narrow interval above it. The municipal assistance service had purchased a licence to explain the first two. The city described the result as efficient allocation. On the hill, where the wind pulled sound through the drainage pipes, the allocation looked completely different.

A young engineer designed a machine that could tell the inhabitants when their streets had become quiet. It listened for forty seconds and then announced that the requisite condition had been achieved.

It was her grandmother who asked why the machine needed to do this out loud.

The engineer altered the system. Whenever the machine had nothing necessary to communicate, it was permitted to stop contributing. She added a switch that prevented it from filling the newly available intervals with reassuring textures.

The switch was difficult to patent because it was not a new way of making a sound. It was a new reason not to.

The planning office demanded an account of the resulting acoustic territory. The engineer submitted two documents: a ledger of the city's loudspeakers and a blank sheet of paper. The second document was returned as incomplete.

She put it back in the envelope.

The next summer, a group of musicians performed in the old tram station. They had written a piece in which all the electronic voices withdrew at different times. At the end a bicycle chain continued to turn somewhere behind the audience. One critic called this the last instrument.

Its owner disagreed. He had been trying to get home.

The musicians did not amend their score to include him.
