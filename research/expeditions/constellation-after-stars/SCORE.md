**I. Before the map:** sound short identical pulses at 0, 3, 6, 10 for four cycles. Do not emphasize any pulse. The carrier remains immutable for movements I–IV.

**II. The appointed constellation:** before audition choose the exact triple {0,1,2}. For each full match, play a contrasting three-note gesture on its positions; otherwise do nothing. This carrier produces no such gesture. Do not change the template after hearing it.

**III. The found constellation:** now permit any equally spaced cyclic triple with spacing 1–5. Deduplicate triples by their three positions. The sole match is {0,3,6}. Play a soft resonant response at those three positions on subsequent repetitions; keep pulse 10 at the original level. The gesture is a new composition, not an audible proof of intention.

**IV. Rejected sky:** audition only the unmatched pulse 10 on a separate bus, without deleting the carrier. Then bring back the whole carrier, then the derived voice alone.

**V. Three skies:** use the executable oracle to enumerate uniform four-onset carriers. Deliberately choose one with zero matches, one with one match and one with four matches from the declared family. Perform all three, retaining four original pulses in each bar. If every match receives a voice, divide the derived gain by the number of simultaneous voices to avoid uncontrolled gain. Announce that these are selected examples, not unconditional random samples.

**VI. The atlas has no verdict:** permit the performer to draw a line through any three positions, including one not in the original family. Label it authored. Allow another performer to decline the new gesture, or to hear the unaltered carrier. Finish without reporting a winner.
