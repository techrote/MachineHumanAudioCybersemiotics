# Two Salaries for One Tune

Original text score; two instruments with identical timbre, one optional witness. No borrowed musical material. Unperformed as a human score; synthetic renderer supplies a demonstrator.

Repeat a twelve-event phrase three times. Each event lasts one quarter second. A/B denote who sounds each event, not the pitch. The phrase may use the twelve frequencies in laboratory.py, or any fixed twelve notes. Keep dynamic, timing, timbre and envelope unchanged between the first four movements. The witness initially hears only the combined sound; explain the allocation before inviting anyone to perform it.

| Movement | Ownership | Intended difference |
|---|---|---|
| I. The needle | ABABABABABAB | Twelve handoffs per cycle. Neither receives two adjacent non-sounding slots. |
| II. The little rooms | AABBAABBAABB | Six handoffs. Both receive three two-slot windows. |
| III. The irregular invoice | ABAAAABBBABB | Six handoffs and the same quota as II. The shortest windows shrink to one. |
| IV. The long room | AAAAAABBBBBB | Two handoffs. Each gets a six-slot window. |

In movement V, **The unassigned attention**, add a second score to each silent window. Mark each as monitoring, preparing, or explicitly relieved. Choose freely. A musician may decline a monitoring demand. The other musician may permit a genuine gap rather than cover it. The invariant musical surface is no longer required. Do not infer rest from silence or consent from participation.

In movement VI, **Audition the invoice**, play the separated stems of II and III, then their mixed versions. If useful, spatialize their ownership lane and listen again. The witness may report a difference, no difference, or something unrelated to the numerical score. Do not require the witness to identify which arrangement was more just or more beautiful.

End when either musician chooses. Do not fill the remaining silence with an explanatory cue.
