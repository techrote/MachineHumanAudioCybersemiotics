## Stop condition and allegations not made

There is **no demonstrated real MHAC case** of an agent laundering a false claim through repetitions in this expedition. Establishing that would require precise proposition selection, dated document versions, traceable source lineage and comparison against primary texts. If that investigation returns transparent primary-source checks or genuine independent observations, log the null; do not invent a pathology for narrative effect.

The provenance count is useful precisely because it has a small meaning. The strongest counterposition is that withholding claims of independent corroboration may eliminate the risky inference without implementing a complex audio provenance protocol. This possibility remains open and can defeat the preferred elaborate design.
