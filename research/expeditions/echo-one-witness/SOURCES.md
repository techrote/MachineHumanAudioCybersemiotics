## Explicit source exclusions and nonclaims

No copyrighted fulltext is imported. No reproduction of Hurlbert's original data, no original Hasher participant data, no full Cochrane systematic-method appraisal and no real-source inclusion/screening decision were performed. No experiment with MHAC listeners, no hardware observation, no agent-confidence survey and no external peer reviewer. The conditional likelihood values in echo_lab.py were constructed **for illustration**; they have no evidential connection to S1–S3's numerical outcomes.

## Source genealogy itself is a potential blind spot

Reading a primary paper's **abstract** does not equal independently verifying its results. A review that cites one paper and another review citing the first do not automatically become two independent studies. But two reports can still contain distinct analyses or primary observations; source tracing should not throw away useful content. This is the exact tension studied here, with no allegation that the cited sources or MHAC have committed an actual violation.

## Reader's checklist

Use the exact accessible locator for each claim; state author report versus original verification separately; distinguish publication from study from sample from source observation; preserve contrary claims; identify new information versus copied statements; and refuse independent corroboration unless its source genealogy and joint dependence assumptions can be evaluated. None of these checks by itself certifies a claim true.
