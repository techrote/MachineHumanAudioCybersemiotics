# The Gallery With an Impossible Third Portrait

**Expedition #564, 25 November 2026.** Original AI-assisted humanities/art research, synthetic exact mathematics and audio; not selected Track E/G evidence, not a production requirement or participant study. Draft/HOLD for review. No license grant or external publication beyond this repository is proposed.

## 1. A commission that cannot be fulfilled

A painter is commissioned to paint a portrait that can be read from three doors. From the first it must be a landscape; from the second a portrait; from the third a completely independent still life. The painter accepts the first two requests but asks how the third door is constructed.

The question is not whether the painter is sufficiently ingenious. It is whether the apparatus grants three independent freedoms. An anamorphic artist composes a relationship between the surface, the viewer and the conditions of viewing. An acoustic artist could compose such relationships for a listener. The third patron may be asking for an impossibility that is concealed by the apparent symmetry of the three doors.

Call this **perspectival counterpoint**: independently write desired works for different listening operators, then compose their unavoidable compromise. The object is the conflict among views, not merely a hidden melody. In the particular two-channel instrument below the views are electrical/digital, not positions in a physical room.

## 3. The three-door contract

Write three synchronized target signals a, b, c for mid, side and left respectively. In a linear two-channel PCM renderer, the actual outputs are m, s, m+s. These are three views of two degrees of freedom. Exact target agreement occurs iff c=a+b at every sample. A third compatible view can still be artistically unexpected; independence of a demand is different from independence of the rendered coordinate.

Let r=c-a-b. Any errors e1=m-a, e2=s-b, e3=(m+s)-c satisfy e1+e2-e3=r. The triangle inequality gives max(|e1|,|e2|,|e3|) >= |r|/3. Equality is attained by m=a+r/3 and s=b+r/3, so the left error is -r/3. For aligned finite signals, the same construction samplewise minimizes the worst absolute sample error over all three views, equal to max|r|/3. It also minimizes the sum of squared errors by orthogonal projection onto the compatibility plane, though the objective is not a psychoacoustic distance. No claim of uniqueness for whole-track max norm is made; quieter residual samples admit other solutions.

Priority policy: satisfy a,b exactly and let the left absorb the full residual. Democratic policy: split the signed error equally. A third policy may preserve the left while letting the other two share the error. Equal sample errors need not be equal perceptual errors, or equally violate a composer's intention.

Generalization: positive weights w1,w2,w3 penalizing absolute errors yield optimum |r|/(1/w1+1/w2+1/w3) for the weighted maximum, before headroom constraints. Choose e1=t sign(r)/w1, e2=t sign(r)/w2, e3=-t sign(r)/w3 at this optimum. This gives a useful authoring control: the right to be exact can be continuously renegotiated. This extension is algebraically derived, not implemented in the present renderer.

Headroom is independent: L=M+S and R=M-S require |M|+|S| <= H for a symmetric sample bound H. This follows from max(|M+S|, |M-S|)=|M|+|S|. Example M=S=24000 is compatible with L=48000 but cannot fit unscaled 16-bit PCM. Neither cancellation nor a good optimizer repairs clipping afterwards. A constrained solver or gain reduction is necessary. Integer division is exact in our renderer because target samples are multiples of three. Arbitrary PCM targets may require rounding and a discrete optimization.

## 4. What the apparatus makes audible (not what the listener hears)

The accompanying original score and standard-library Python script create three independent eight-event melodies, in common 0.4-second slots, with different harmonic partial weights. The priority stereo file realizes the first two targets exactly; its left channel is their sum, rather than the third melody. The compromise file is an alternative stereo master. Direct mono exports expose the prescribed and actual views at fixed sample gain. No adaptive normalization, psychoacoustic inference, acoustic spatial measurement or private-channel security is asserted.

The independent integer search in laboratory.py examines 729 scalar triples in {-12,-9,...,12} with candidate m,s from -24..24. It tests the exact minimax bound and equal signed discrepancy; a feasible-but-overrange negative case is retained. Every one of 51,200 audio frames is checked for the matrix round trip and the exact error bound. Nine WAV files are rendered twice; sample bounds, payload and header readback and hash repeatability are asserted. See receipt.txt for native execution output. No CI or human audition claim.

**Artistic objection.** A compromise may destroy all three works instead of preserving three recognizable fragments. It can be preferable to leave one intact and sacrifice another. A listener may be interested in the very unstable interference rather than recovering the three intended melodies. The model has no basis for declaring one of these preferences correct.

**Technical objection.** Give the artist a third physical/digital channel, a nonlinear selector, or different time slots and the three-demand incompatibility may vanish. That is not a refutation; it changes the instrument contract. Even two channels can hold arbitrary encoded data using temporal/frequency multiplexing, but those decoders are not the three stipulated simultaneous linear views. These are deliberately narrow equalities, not information-theoretic limits on stereo in general.

## 5. Exaptation: the Anamorph Organ

Build an inverse-mixing sequencer with a target editor for each named listening transform; an explicit matrix; a constraint compiler; signed residual lanes; and independent audition of target, realized and discrepancy tracks. A performer could lock a view, drag its priority, or move through a family of view operators. Keep gain, latency, headroom and phase/polarity conventions explicit. The residual need not be an error in the aesthetic sense; it may drive FM, resonator excitation, or control the rate of transitions between views.

An ambitious multi-agent variant: distribute many oscillator grains across a fixed set of synthesis buses. Each artist authors an intended score at one projection. Where demanded views conflict, assign the mismatch to a separate modulation channel or choose which local view has priority in each time-frequency tile. Transitions must be bounded and discontinuities auditioned; an independent residual bus is an extra channel, not an achievement of the original constraint. DAWs, matrix routing and constrained optimization are adequate prior art for all of the core engineering. The distinctive idea is authoring the inconsistent commission as the score.

For MHAC, a related but unvalidated hypothesis is that a device's normal stereo cue, its mono fold-down, and an explicitly decoded side diagnostic might be specified independently although the actual linear rendering couples them. Compare ordinary single-view design, compatibility-aware authoring and a straightforward separate diagnostic channel on synthetic cue sets; report exact waveform error, headroom violations, and actual task correctness only after approved human work. A null result (ordinary direct representation wins) is useful. Never hide essential actions in a side-only channel or assume that equal sample errors are semantically equitable. This experiment does not alter the central auditory protocol.

### Fictional coda: the third patron

The gallery commissioned three paintings and purchased two surfaces. The conservator, not wishing to disappoint a patron, invented a third doorway and gave it an engraved brass nameplate. The third visitor found the sum of the other two pictures and complained that her own portrait had been promised.

The painter proposed moving the two existing portraits by a little, and the third appeared, also moved by a little. The director declared that each patron had been equally disappointed.

A musician asked the patrons what they had actually lost. One had lost a colour, one a name, and one the face of a person she remembered. The director showed them three identical numbers.

At the opening the painter placed the numbers on a separate stand and labelled them *measurements of displacement*. The three visitors argued about the pictures. The stand remained silent.
