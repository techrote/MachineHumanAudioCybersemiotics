# Three Patrons, Two Surfaces — original performance instructions

For an electronic performer and three score writers, or one performer playing all roles. Duration may be extended; the provided rendered miniature is 3.2 seconds per track. No external audio is required.

**I. The commissions.** Each writer supplies an eight-slot melody. In the supplied example pitch offsets from 220 Hz are A: 0,4,7,9,7,4,2,0; B: 12,9,5,2,5,9,12,14; C: 7,7,10,5,3,10,3,7. Each slot lasts 0.4 s. Introduce each alone, with labels. These are three independent target scores, not a stereo master.

**II. The first two patrons.** Encode A as mid and B as side; play the stereo master, then explicitly decode the mid, side and left views. Let the third composer hear the left view alongside C. Do not tell the audience that an unprocessed stereo listening position is a digital side decoder. The two exact target tracks can also be played directly.

**III. The equal displacement.** Play the compromise stereo master, then audition its three corresponding mono views. Keep gain fixed between target and realized comparisons. Each sample discrepancy is one third of the signed incompatibility, with signs as described in the essay. Allow the three writers to judge the results separately without voting.

**IV. The extra room.** Add a third independent output or a sequential third slot, explicitly breaking the earlier contract. Play the third target exactly. Alternatively refuse the extra room, keep one view intact and deliberately abandon another. The unperformed score does not require a declaration that the mathematical compromise is artistically successful.

Use moderate playback levels and ordinary speakers or headphones. The side solo is an explicit digital subtraction, not a secret binaural channel. The source renderer uses original oscillators, no borrowed recordings. No human performance or listening trial has been conducted.
