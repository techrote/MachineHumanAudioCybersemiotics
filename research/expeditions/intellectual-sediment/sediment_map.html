Most defensible mechanism

Stigmergic research ecology.

An action leaves a trace in a shared medium; later work encounters that trace and changes. Git issues, branches, source records and fixtures provide transparency, provenance and experimentability without requiring a continuous private subject.

Direct prompts, RAG routing, shared training and human selection remain major alternative/parallel mechanisms.

The practical by-product

The recurring operator can be made operational: find two quantities being collapsed → construct a divergence witness → represent them separately → test whether the split changes action/inference → retain the simpler collapse if no discriminating case exists.

distinction_miner.py retrieves candidate prior separations with file/line provenance. quotient_guard.py checks a finite state table for collapsed states requiring different declared decisions. Neither tool discovers truth or ontology automatically.
