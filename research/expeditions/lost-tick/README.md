# The Clock That Loses a Tick

Refs #775, #750, #767, #771 · 27 November 2026. Exploratory mathematical DSP outside frozen Track E/G. One ChatGPT agent derived, implemented, cross-checked and wrote this expedition; no independent peer review, novelty/priority claim, participant evidence or physical qualification.

## The anomaly: recovering the clock is not recovering its history

The earlier [#771](https://github.com/techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics/issues/771) weighted-alibi decoder assumes coherent sample indices. A dropped sample changes the indexing of every later observation rather than simply altering one amplitude. Here a trusted waveform template is available (or reconstructed beforehand from an independently trusted prefix). We can recover a settled phase offset reliably even when the exact historical deletion position is permanently ambiguous. These are two different inference tasks.

Let x(t)=b+sum_i f_i(t mod p_i), with distinct pairwise coprime periods p_i>1 and arbitrary real private shapes; require x nonconstant. Take one deletion with candidate boundary k: z_k(t)=x(t) for t<k and x(t+1) otherwise, over t=0,...,N-1. k=N represents no visible deletion in this window. This is an integer sample loss model, not continuous sample-rate drift or fractional jitter. Substitution errors replace sample values arbitrarily; flagged erasures retain their indices. The template is exact and shared across hypotheses. In particular, these results do not simultaneously learn an unknown waveform from corrupted samples.

## Proof: the clock-slip distance

For k<l, the predictions differ only on [k,l). Their Hamming distance equals the number of nonzero values of h(t)=x(t+1)-x(t) on that interval. With at most e substitutions and f flagged erasures per explanation, common received observations exist exactly when this distance is <=2e+f (when erasure locations may be chosen adversarially). Necessity follows from the triangle inequality on surviving coordinates. For sufficiency erase up to f differing coordinates, then partition the remaining <=2e disagreements into two groups of size <=e; choose received values from one prediction on the first group and the other on the second. Extra positions may be erased if a fixed erasure count is required and enough samples exist. For a specified erasure mask, use the distance restricted to surviving coordinates and threshold 2e instead.

The difference h belongs to the direct sum W of zero-mean private periodic spaces of dimension d=sum_i(p_i-1). Conversely every h in W has an additive periodic primitive x: integrate each private component around its period, whose zero sum guarantees consistency. Distinct nontrivial Fourier modes of pairwise coprime periods imply that any d consecutive h values determine h completely (invertible Fourier/Vandermonde evaluation); equivalently, they satisfy the monic recurrence Q(E)h=0, with Q(z)=product_i (1+z+...+z^(p_i-1)), degree d. Its constant coefficient is one, so the recurrence works backwards as well. It annihilates exactly W: the recurrence space and W have the same dimension and inclusion holds.

For nonconstant x, h is not identically zero, hence cannot vanish on d consecutive ticks. The maximum noiseless separation of two indistinguishable deletion boundaries is therefore exactly d-1: choose initial h=(0,...,0,1) and integrate. This is an actual plateau of d identical x samples, not a numerical near-tie. General fault budgets reduce to the minimum nonzero Hamming weight of the N-tick restriction of W. Let L_r be the smallest positive N for which every nonzero h has more than r nonzero observations in that window. Then the greatest possible separation between compatible deletion boundaries is L_r-1 for r=2e+f. Necessity holds for every template and location; sharpness is over the class of templates, not every individual waveform. Translation preserves W. At smaller lengths, the sharp counterexample is a nonzero h with weight <=r and its periodic primitive. If r is finite, L_r exists because every d consecutive ticks contain a nonzero value. The crude bound L_r<=d(r+1) follows by splitting into disjoint d-tick blocks.

Exact event localization under one substitution is impossible uniformly even with a very rapidly changing template: two adjacent deletion boundaries differ at most at one observation. A longer recording can exclude remote candidates but cannot retroactively change that one disagreement. The valid receiver output is a candidate set, not necessarily a unique boundary or even a contiguous interval.

## The five-prime certificate and the unexpected four-column obstruction

For periods (5,7,11,13,17), d=48. For N=d+s let H_s have s rows of shifted coefficients of Q, i.e. H_s[j,i]=Q[i-j], zero outside 0..d. Its kernel is exactly the restricted difference code, because the monic recurrence fixes the remaining values from the first d and the check rows are independent. Minimum distance >r iff every <=r columns are independent, provided N>=d. Any nonzero codeword at a longer length retains a nonzero prefix of length at least d, and extending the window cannot decrease distance.

At N=48 the distance is 1, since the first 48 values are arbitrary. At 49 it is 2, because every Q coefficient is strictly positive and two columns of a single-row check are dependent. At 50 all 1,225 two-column determinants are nonzero, so distance is 3 (the upper bound s+1 follows by choosing s+1 columns). At 51 all 20,825 three-column determinants are nonzero, so distance is 4. At 52, of all 270,725 four-column determinants, exactly four vanish:

| Zero-based support | Exact nonzero dependence | Fifth-row inner product |
|---|---|---:|
| (1,4,5,6) | (-5,7,-7,2) | 2 |
| (2,4,5,9) | (-49,64,-35,1) | 14 |
| (42,46,47,49) | (1,-35,64,-49) | -50 |
| (45,46,47,50) | (2,-7,7,-5) | -7 |

These are real rational dependencies, not modular coincidences. The first 51 samples certify distance >=4, so distance at 52 is exactly 4. At 53 the fifth check row resolves every exceptional support (each old triple is independent, so each exceptional four-column kernel is one dimensional). A support involving the newly appended column is independent: the first four rows force its other at most three coefficients to vanish, and the fifth forces the new coefficient to vanish. Thus distance at 53 is at least 5; no claim of its exact distance beyond that is made.

| 0 | 0 | 0 | 48 | 47 |

Other (e,f) pairs with the same r have the same thresholds. In particular two arbitrary substitutions do **not** admit the dimension-plus-four deadline: the distance stalls at four for one extra tick. This is a direct, finite exact failure of a generic maximum-distance assumption.

The laboratory integrates explicit sharp short-prefix differences: length 47 all zero followed by a nonzero tick; length 48 support {0}, amplitude 1; length 49 support {0,1}, amplitudes (5,-1); length 50 support {0,1,2}, amplitudes (10,-5,1); length 52 support {1,4,5,6}, amplitudes (-5,7,-7,2). Each is extended by the exact recurrence. The native laboratory independently produces explicit periodic primitive tables by exact rational inversion of the actual contrast matrix. Those tables are small integers, so the ambiguity is not an artifact of immense dynamic range. For the two-error 52-tick witness, the receiver actually retains every boundary from 0 through 52 inclusive: 53 compatible historical accounts. The first and last boundary remain observationally compatible even with arbitrarily long clean tails, since their predictions are identical thereafter.

## Recover settled phase, abstain about exact event time

Suppose instead that a tail is already known to have a constant index offset s in {-1,0,1}. Candidate predictions x(t+s) and x(t+s') differ by a member of W. For the five odd primes, neither a shift difference of 1 nor 2 is a multiple of any p_i, so the operator E^s-E^s' is invertible on W: on each nontrivial Fourier root its multiplier is nonzero. Consequently every nonconstant x gives a nonzero difference for any two distinct candidates; every h in W is also realizable as such a difference for some x. Thus the exact thresholds L_r in the table are also sharp worst-case tail lengths for identifying the offset, including the no-slip offset, given known template, settled tail, coherent indices within the tail and the stipulated faults. Sharpness follows by taking a sparse h and inverting the shift-difference operator. For general periods or a wider offset vocabulary, distinct shifts can coincide for waveforms supported on components whose periods divide their difference; qualify the actual nonzero modes before making a universal claim.

This is the useful distinction: phase recovery can be certified after 50 tail samples with one substitution, while the historical slip time may still have 50 compatible candidate boundaries (0..49). The tail qualification does not tell the receiver when the settled tail began. If the template phase itself was never anchored, an overall shift of the private phases is a gauge ambiguity. A constant waveform provides no timing information at all.

The exact list receiver needs one pass to count mismatches for boundary zero and then changes just one contribution per candidate step. Initialize C(0)=sum of surviving mismatch indicators against x(t+1); update C(k+1)=C(k)+1[z(k)!=x(k)]-1[z(k)!=x(k+1)] at a nonerased k; otherwise leave C unchanged. Retain C(k)<=e. Runtime O(N) after template samples are available; storing the complete candidate list may use O(N) space, while retaining only its extremes uses O(1) space. Do not label an interval hull as a complete candidate set if holes exist. Return empty set as model inconsistency, singleton as exact conditional identification, multiple candidates as an ambiguity receipt. This is an exact-equality reference implementation; quantization and small dense noise are separate contracts.

A final negative control: take the 47-tick zero-difference witness, delete at zero and then duplicate the sample at the end of the excursion. The index offset is +1 for 47 ticks and then zero again. Every observed amplitude equals the unslipped template; the paired event is entirely invisible even to an infinite noiseless continuation. This is a deterministic counterexample, not a measured incidence of actual audio clock errors.
