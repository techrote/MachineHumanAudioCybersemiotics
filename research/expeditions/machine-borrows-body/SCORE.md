# Four Bodies for Six Impacts

Original audiovisual performance score, 25 November 2026. Use the standalone apparatus or an equivalent fixed recording and independent visual player. The score is unperformed.

## I. The carpenter's contract

Play the soundtrack with the lever at zero lag. Repeat with the sphere, then with the shutter. The sound operator may not change the recording. The visual operator is free to vary the appearance of the object between repetitions, but not its marked contact times. Do not ask the audience to identify a correct material; the work does not have one.

## II. The late body

Keep the soundtrack fixed. Shift the visual action later by 150 ms, then earlier by 150 ms. The audience may choose to attend to the sound, the movement, or their disagreement. Neither alignment is privileged by the score. The editor's delay is a chosen compositional number, not a perceptual threshold.

## III. The missing movement

Omit the fourth visual contact while keeping its sound. Repeat with a different omission. Finally remove the body entirely. The sound is not an alarm and need not be explained. In a separate reprise the visual operator may perform without sound.

## IV. The workshop behind the workshop

Display the sound's actual method of production: two decaying oscillatory terms and seeded noise. This diagram must not move in imitation of the lever. The audience can now inspect two separate layers: a production account and a fictional depiction. End by withdrawing both.

## Speculative interlude — the registrar of surfaces

The municipal theatre owned one noise and three small machines. A carpenter lent it a hinge. A physicist lent it a sphere. A photographer lent it a shutter.

Each object was issued with the same six appointments. The noise arrived at every appointment. An inspector observed the hinge and certified that the hinge had spoken.

The carpenter opened its casing. There was no speaker inside.

The theatre did not return the hinge. It added a small sign beside it: PERFORMER, NOT WITNESS.

At the next concert the theatre borrowed a body that could not move at all. Its appointments were kept in a separate book. The composer tore out one page and the noise continued.

The inspector requested a second signature on the report. The registrar pointed to the same clock on both desks.
