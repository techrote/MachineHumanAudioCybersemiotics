## Model and result

A labelled cyclic binary array x of length n>=3 is updated by the median of left, self, right. Snapshot mode reads all old values; serial mode changes one eligible site at a time until no change is possible. No-op updates are ignored. An eligible site is initially isolated: both neighbours have its opposite value. Let E be the induced subgraph of these sites on the cycle.

**Theorem 1.** Reachable serial terminal states are in bijection with maximal independent sets S of E, via flipping exactly the sites in S. Any ordering of S is a valid effective schedule.

Proof: mark equal adjacent pairs. An equal pair can never be broken by a single-site median update because each member has at least one equal neighbour. A flip at an isolated site creates two equal pairs and destroys none; it disables itself and its immediate neighbours and cannot make any site eligible. Thus all flipped sites were originally eligible, no two are adjacent, and termination is precisely domination of E. Conversely any independent set can be flipped in any order, and maximality guarantees no eligible site survives. The endpoint uniquely determines the flipped set. Each effective flip removes exactly two disagreeing edges. This proof does not assume a random scheduler.

For an alternating even cycle E is the whole cycle, giving exactly the nth Perrin number of labelled terminal states (known maximal-independent-set count, not new enumeration theory). Flipping one parity produces all zeros, flipping the other all ones, while snapshot updates oscillate indefinitely between complementary alternating states. Min/max effective flip counts on this cycle are ceil(n/3) and n/2: successive chosen sites have cyclic gaps 2 or 3, and every integer gap decomposition of n into 2s and 3s yields a maximal independent set. One need not use a larger radius or different local operation to produce different endpoints.

**Theorem 2.** For a nonalternating initial array, let L be the length of its longest run of initially eligible sites (0 if none). Snapshot mode reaches a fixed point after ceil(L/2) updates. Its endpoint is serial-reachable iff no eligible run has length congruent to 2 modulo 4.

Proof: equal adjacent pairs are permanent in snapshot mode too. Inside each maximal isolated run, the two stable boundaries advance inward one cell per update; all not-yet-absorbed cells still have two opposite neighbours and flip. Induction gives absorption time of position j (1-based in a run of length k) equal to min(j,k+1-j); at that time it is permanently equal to its outside neighbour. For an even run the last two cells both flip and each joins the respective stable side. Thus the final flipped subset consists of sites at odd distance from the closer boundary. It dominates the run (the gaps between flipped positions are at most three and both endpoints are selected). Consecutive flipped positions can occur only at the two central cells of an even run, and occur exactly when k/2 is odd. By Theorem 1 this violates serial reachability exactly for k=2 mod 4. Distinct runs are separated by initially stable sites. For example 001011 -> 000111 in one snapshot update, but its only serial terminal states are 000011 and 001111. The snapshot result has three ones, while serial endpoints have two and four.

## Compiler

median.cjs exports compile(x), step(x), verify(). compile returns one bin per attainable terminal number of ones: exact count (decimal string), minimum effective flip count and one legal witness schedule achieving that minimum. Fix the first two flip indicators, scan left to right remembering the last two indicators and signed change in population. A triple is admissible iff its centre is either unchosen or an eligible site with unchosen neighbours, and every eligible centre is covered by itself or a neighbour. Close both wraparound triples at the end. Aggregate counts with BigInt, and retain the lowest flip-count witness per state. A scan has O(n^2) states across all layers, O(n) rolling entries, excluding the O(n)-length stored witness strings and arbitrary-precision cost. Bound n<=128. Counts are distinct labelled endpoints, NOT update sequence counts, probabilities or rotation equivalence classes. To run: node median.cjs. No packages needed.

## Qualification

The exact compiler was executed in a V8 isolate; an independent bitwise state-transition oracle recursively enumerated every reachable terminal state for all 8,184 inputs of lengths 3..12. Compared counts, min costs and replayed 16,138 legal witnesses. 18,036 input/terminal pairs; 4,894 inputs have multiple serial terminals; 2,654 have a snapshot fixed point that cannot be reached serially; 10 initial states oscillate under snapshot. All snapshot time/reachability criteria checked. Perrin counts and all-zero/all-one extreme witnesses checked for every even n from 4 to 128. Four invalid inputs rejected. At 128 cells the alternating input has 4,283,494,371,512,410 labelled terminal states. Separate native Python tuple-transition recursion reproduced the five aggregate counts. Same agent, independent implementations, not independent human review.
