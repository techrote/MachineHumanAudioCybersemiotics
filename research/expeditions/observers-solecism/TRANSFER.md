# Exaptation ledger: where this could wander next

**Purpose:** preserve plausible cross-domain transfers without forcing a new implementation programme or claiming scientific validation. Refs [MHAC-X001 #178](https://github.com/techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics/issues/178).

## What is directly established

For the authored finite N-probe sampling operator, different continuous circle harmonics produce identical sensor data. A particular shifted position distinguishes the default two-mode pair; a moving probe produces two deterministic audio-frequency traces. This is mathematics and synthesis, not a human listener study.

The factorisation theorem additionally formalises when a property of a full state can be recovered from a partial observation. No agreement between theorems and human meaning has been established.

## Synthesizer architectures worth preserving

### Observer-orbit oscillator

Build a performable instrument in which multiple virtual probes orbit a hidden cyclic wavetable. The player controls:
- probe positions, number of stations, phase offsets, rotation rates, wrapping and spatial interpolation;
- the underlying harmonic field, mixture of modes and boundary periodicity;
- left/right or ambisonic rendering, independent grain windows, detuning and filter envelopes;
- a transition between discrete stationary probes and continuous motion;
- a visible **alias ledger** explaining which different field configurations currently generate identical outputs at the chosen probes.

It offers a nonstandard musical action: **move the observer rather than editing the oscillator**. This is an original design proposal; no third-party architecture or sonic preference study is claimed.

### Counterfactual instrument panel

Allow a player to ask "what hidden mode would produce the same current readings?" Show several compatible field hypotheses. Move a virtual probe, revealing differences. Sound could accompany the expanding distinction. For the default harmonic pair, the proof in MATHEMATICS.md even identifies the single most informative next probe at about 17.35°, demonstrating that **active choice of where to observe** can matter more than adding confidence to a fixed grid.

Importantly, this is not a proof that any particular real-world source contains such a hidden mode, and listening would not by itself certify an epistemic repair.

### Semantic patchbay

An exploratory interface could let user-defined meaning fields enter as tagged channels—urgency, uncertainty, object identity, action—with all mappings explicitly declared. A separate "missing witness" switch shows what is no longer recoverable after a data channel is dropped.

**Boundary:** MHAC-T009 already investigates multivariate acoustic mappings, and MHAC-T004 binding/compositional syntax; future work must collaborate rather than duplicate those experiments. Do not imply arbitrary waveform parameters support intuitive, accurate human action.

## HCI, linguistic and agentic connections

- **Grammatical judgement:** a grammatical form can be evaluated against a context and a norm, not merely against an isolated spelling. However ambiguity, error and dialect variation must remain distinct.
- **Error messages:** a system may report "invalid" when actually meaning "required context was unavailable." An interface could distinguish *contradicted*, *unknown*, *underdetermined* and *incompatible*.
- **Agent appeasement:** emotional wording could alter the assistant's output even when the technical problem is fixed. This may be considered a contextual measurement/response effect, but our cosine proof establishes nothing about models or emotions. See [T018 #177](https://github.com/techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics/issues/177).
- **Research archaeology:** a speculative aliasing metaphor may later become a pragmatic design for probing an underinstrumented system; this is an exaptation opportunity, not a promised breakthrough.

## Explicitly rejected overclaims

1. "We have proved that human semantic meaning has compact extra dimensions." No.
2. "Quintilian anticipated Kaluza–Klein theory." No.
3. "A receiver can always recover a hidden message by moving its sensors." No; only the chosen pair with an appropriate new observation.
4. "Two PCM channels at different frequencies establish listener discriminability, urgency or correct action." No.
5. "The sound demonstrates mass spectra of Kaluza–Klein particles." No; probe rotation rate is an artistic parameter.
6. "All grammatical errors are incomplete-observer problems." No.
7. "The expedition completes MHAC-R008 or formally admits sources." No.

## Next speculative trails

- **The topology of disagreement:** find tasks where participants' classifications are each correct relative to distinct context variables, and study which *counterfactual question* best distinguishes them.
- **The sound of missing context:** make continuous music that becomes ambiguous at deliberately sparse spatial/temporal samples; investigate whether disambiguating motion creates interesting performative gestures.
- **Post-human metacommunication:** write an explicitly fictional ontology in which grammaticality is defined relative to observer transforms rather than languages; search for contradictions and places where ordinary pragmatics explains it better.
- **Exact visual field cross-over:** render observation fibres as animated line fields or interference bands in an *independent* graphics project, only if later deliberately commissioned. Do not write another repo from MHAC.
- **Mathematical edge-case atlas:** extend the exact alias proof to uneven probe placements, frequency-bounded reconstruction, noisy observations and adaptive selection, keeping source-evidence status explicit.

## Decision

Retain the experiment as an original example of the kind of intellectual sediment MHAC was designed to accumulate. It should be free to seed music, art, HCI or further philosophy without a claim that those directions were in the original audio review.

**No task or tool in this note authorises participant experiments, new schedules, a mandatory research framework, rewriting the frozen protocol, or cross-repository mutations.**
