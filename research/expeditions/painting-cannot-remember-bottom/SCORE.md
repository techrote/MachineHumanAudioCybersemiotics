# Six Windows for an Underpainting

Original performance score, 25 November 2026. For four sound layers, an underpainter, and an excavator. The sound material is original to this synthetic study; no adaptation of Cage's score is intended.

Set A,B,C,D to tones of 196, 293.66, 440, 587.33 Hz respectively, each with a quiet harmonic of index 2,3,4,5. The digital miniature uses half-second phase-reset sin-squared envelopes. Each audition mask selects the highest eligible layer in the current order. In live performance the layer identities may be more elaborate motifs; all performers must be able to distinguish their identities for the formal game, but an audience need not.

## I — The portrait
Use priority A>B>C>D. The six windows are ABCD, BCD, ABC, ABD, AB, BCD. Perform A B A A A B. Close the inspection without showing the bottom layers.

## II — The city built afterwards
Change the priority to A>B>D>C. Repeat all six windows without changing any tone. The performed surface is identical. Do not imply the change represents historical order.

## III — The conservator's descent
Restore the first order. Open a window containing the entire inventory. After each half-second, exclude its winner from the next window. The last layer is known from the inventory; audition it as an extra artistic gesture, not a necessary diagnostic probe. Perform A B C D.

## IV — The second descent
Use the second order. Repeat the adaptive rule. Perform A B D C.

## V — Two disputed layers
With only C and D admitted, play the uppermost in the first painting, then the uppermost in the second. Let an interval of silence separate them. The generated bottom-a/b recordings are independent half-second fragments; the silence and joining are a performance choice.

## VI — Refusal of the final window
The underpainter may refuse to reveal an additional, newly composed layer. Do not claim the resulting silence proves an empty archive. The audience may elect to end the performance. This movement is a written instruction only.

The first four movements and two fragments of V are rendered by laboratory.py. No live performance or listening trial occurred. For a real collaboration, make inspection permissions and destructive deletion explicit; the score's fictional refusal is not an authorization mechanism.
