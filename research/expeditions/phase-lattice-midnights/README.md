## Anomaly and precise scope

The prior [Chord That Outruns Its Correlator](https://github.com/techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics/issues/412) already proved the general lattice criterion for periodic correlation completeness, sharp two-tone thresholds, and a special two-dimensional rank-to-saturation result. This expedition explicitly extends that sediment: a full-real-rank, connected cubic-relation system can have exponentially many discrete nontranslation alternatives. The new construction also provides a constant-order multiscale repair with a uniform separation margin. No claim that the lattice criterion itself is new.

Consider real periodic trigonometric polynomials with known positive magnitudes on positive integer harmonic support S, conjugates at negative frequencies, and fixed zero DC. All phases are in turns (modulo 1). Physical translations act by theta_s -> theta_s + s*t. The continuous circle is the nuisance group, not an integer sample clock. Products use integer zero frequency sums, not modular wraparound. The power spectrum and full complex bispectrum are the observations, not merely their magnitudes. Every nontrivial cubic product is a permutation or conjugate of F_a F_b conjugate(F_(a+b)), for positive a,b,a+b in S. Zero entries outside S carry no phase evidence.

For S={3,5,6,10,12,20,24,30}, the complete list of positive cubic triples is (3,3,6), (5,5,10), (6,6,12), (6,24,30), (10,10,20), (10,20,30), (12,12,24). The hypergraph is connected and the seven rows have real rank seven. Nevertheless the entire bispectrum and powers are identical for the all-zero phase score and the phase score (in S order) (1/10,0,2/10,0,4/10,0,8/10,0). No time shift maps one into the other. Their invariant four-factor product F_3 F_12 conjugate(F_5 F_10) has opposite signs. This is a discrete alternative, not an unmeasured continuous phase dial.

## Integer-lattice certificate

Let m=|S| and s be the frequency vector. For each positive cubic triple form row e_a+e_b-e_(a+b), including coefficient 2 for a=b. Let L be the integer span of these rows, contained in K={v in Z^m : v dot s=0}. Equal power and bispectrum imply that the phase difference alpha satisfies every row equation modulo 1; the converse is immediate from the full list of nonzero products.

Smith normal form gives nonzero diagonal integers d_1,...,d_R of the row matrix. The phase-difference group is Hom(Z^m/L,R/Z), isomorphic to a torus of dimension m-R and a product of cyclic groups of orders d_i. Write g=gcd(S). The homomorphism v -> v dot (s/g) maps onto Z, so its kernel K splits off a free rank-one complement (choose an integer vector with dot product 1 by Bezout). The translation circle is precisely the character group of that free summand, and quotienting by it gives Hom(K/L,R/Z). Thus there are m-R-1 continuous degrees of freedom and finite factors d_i. When R=m-1 the number of inequivalent phase configurations for any feasible complete receipt is exactly product d_i. Real rank alone checks only the dimension, not integer saturation. This is a restatement/quantification of the general criterion in #412 using Smith normal form, not a novelty claim.

## Exponential discrete ambiguity: constructive theorem

For integer r>=0 take the support

    S_r = {3*2^i : 0<=i<=r+2} union {5*2^i : 0<=i<=r+1} union {15*2^r}.

The 2r+6 positive carriers consist of two doubling chains meeting at a single terminal frequency. There are no cross-chain sums: a 3-chain plus a 5-chain sum is divisible by neither 3 nor 5, so cannot be in S. A same-chain sum cannot land in the other chain (the latter is not divisible by the first chain's prime). A sum within one doubling chain is another power of two times the base only when the summands coincide. The terminal frequency is greater than every other member; its only representations are 3*(2^r+2^(r+2)) and 5*(2^r+2^(r+1)), by uniqueness of binary expansion. Thus the entire nontrivial cubic dictionary is exactly the two chains and these two terminal equations.

Every phase-difference solution is determined by the two seed phases x=alpha_3 and y=alpha_5. Along each chain phases double. At the terminal the equation is

    2^r (5x-3y) = 0 mod 1.

Eliminating the intermediate carriers leaves one primitive linear relation multiplied by 2^r. Its real rank is one; with the eliminated variables, total rank is 2r+5=m-1. The hypergraph is connected. The invariant q=5x-3y has exactly 2^r possible values j/2^r. For a fixed q, the difference of two seed pairs is a time shift: the kernel of (5,-3) on the two-torus is the image of t -> (3t,5t), since 3 and 5 are coprime. This can also be verified explicitly using Bezout (2*3-5=1). Thus there are exactly 2^r distinct phase classes, each represented by x=j/(5*2^r), y=0, with terminal phase zero. The group is cyclic, not a collection of independent binary choices. The case r=0 has only one class.

This gives 4096 phase classes from 30 carriers at r=12. The maximum frequency grows as 15*2^r: exponential in the number of carriers is NOT exponential in a fixed physical bandwidth. There is no conflict with the two-tone rank/saturation theorem of #412 or dense-support bispectral completeness.

## Fourth-order repair and robustness

One extra translation-invariant four-factor receipt suffices:

    Q_i = F_(3*2^i) F_(12*2^i) conjugate(F_(5*2^i) F_(10*2^i)),
    i=0,...,r.

The positive frequency sums on either side are 15*2^i. Divide by the product of the four known magnitudes to normalize. Relative to a reference phase solution, Q_i changes by exp(2*pi*i_complex*2^i*q). (Here the subscript i is the scale index; i_complex is sqrt(-1).) Q_0 directly reveals q, so the augmented integer row lattice is saturated. No product of order <=3 can do this for r>=1, as all such products were already observed. Hence the exact minimum complete correlation order is four for this family, assuming power already supplied; the result concerns one extra selected complex product, not a full dense fourth-order tensor.

But the normalized Q_0 values are equally spaced roots of unity; their minimum complex Euclidean distance is 2 sin(pi/2^r). A bounded-error nearest-codeword classifier for this exact fibre has sharp open error-ball radius sin(pi/2^r). Exact recovery from one new receipt therefore becomes numerically fragile.

A multiscale repair costs r fourth-order receipts Q_0,...,Q_(r-1) (Q_r is redundant). For any distinct classes j,k, write their nonzero difference as 2^v*u with u odd and v<r. At scale i=r-v-1, the difference in turns is an odd half-integer. The normalized receipts are antipodes. Every pair of codewords consequently has maximum-coordinate complex distance EXACTLY 2, the largest possible for unit phasors. Absolute error strictly less than 1 in every normalized receipt suffices to decode the class by minimum maximum-coordinate distance; at error 1 the midpoint of two codewords is compatible with both. This is a sharp worst-case guarantee for the conditional fibre with exact cubic measurements, exact magnitudes and additive complex-coordinate errors. It is NOT a global bound with uncertain cubic phase constraints or unnormalized noisy signal measurements.

Within this particular scaled-receipt dictionary every one of those r scales is necessary to achieve separation strictly greater than sqrt(2). If scale i is omitted, choose difference 2^(r-i-1). All higher scales then agree, all lower scales have chord distance at most sqrt(2), and the omitted scale alone was antipodal. This does not prove a global lower bound over arbitrary fourth-order or higher-order measurements. Also the Hamming distance against whole-record corruptions is just 1: j and j+2^(r-1) differ only at Q_0. Numerical separation is not whole-receipt fault correction.

This is an actual digital Fourier feature realization, not just a symbolic sonification; no claim of exact PCM bispectral equality, physical acquisition, or listener discrimination. Both native runs produced identical execution.json and WAV hashes. Receipt and audio can be regenerated; audio is not committed. Full repository validation is separate.

Design hypothesis: an invariant acoustic receiver should audit the saturation of its actual frequency relation lattice, not just real matrix rank, connectivity or a successful local optimizer. A phase-dial synthesizer could play any of these hidden phase classes, expose Q_0 as a fine phase dial, and add the multiscale bank as a conditional discriminator. The raw chord and a sonification of its invariant receipts could form two performance voices. The offline chord renderer is implemented; the live instrument is speculative. Raw time-domain observations or a trustworthy clock can circumvent the invariant-identification problem entirely. Stronger higher-order statistics may be expensive or noisy; a weaker spectral magnitude reduces unnormalized receipt amplitude. All phase relations here are deterministic and time-stationary; fluctuating channels, finite windows, Nyquist aliases, and stochastic cumulants are different contracts. Zero DC is fixed, not inferred; positive waveforms can instead be obtained by a common sufficiently large DC without adding phase constraints.

Original fiction: a bellmaker lends a clerk two clocks. Their gears meet at the same midnight, and every local gear ratio passes inspection. Yet their calendars differ by a hidden half-turn. The apprentice compares two pairs of gears at a smaller scale. The clerk learns that an agreed midnight did not fix the date.
