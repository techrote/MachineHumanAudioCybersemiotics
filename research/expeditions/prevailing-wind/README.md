# The Instrument With a Prevailing Wind

*Directional resistance as a compositional material.* 2026-11-24. Exploratory essay / synthetic construction / performance research proposal; not a contribution to the frozen evidence corpus.

## A piano in which the notes have weather (original interpretation)

Imagine four patches arranged around a circular controller. They always produce the same four sounds. Every patch can still lead to either neighbour. Yet moving clockwise has become easy and moving anticlockwise expensive. An archivist saves the patches, the mapping, even the adjacency graph. She loses the weather.

The weather is neither a fifth sound nor a new rule forbidding any of the old sounds. It is a modification of the relationships between actions. A musician can author timbre while another authors what is inviting, awkward or costly to do next. The two authors need not agree. An interesting patch might be worth approaching from an inconvenient direction.

Call this directionally weighted instrument space, or, for the artistic work, a prevailing wind. Its object is not the inaccessible note but the unequal journeys to an equally accessible note. The term is a metaphor: nothing here simulates a fluid or establishes a measure of human effort.

## Situated readings and counterpositions

- **Lachenmann:** Martin Kaltenecker's [IRCAM survey of Helmut Lachenmann](https://resources.ircam.fr/en/composer/helmut-lachenmann/workcourse), updated 2022-02-23, paragraphs beginning “À la fin des années 1960” and “Il s'agira”, describes musique concrète instrumentale and quotes Lachenmann on audible conditions of production, gesture and mechanical resistance. Read the French paragraphs, not the original German essays or the entire oeuvre. This is a secondary source transmitting the composer's statements. The inversion proposed here is to compose an initially *inaudible* condition of production and optionally expose it as a second voice. A synthetic oscillator does not thereby acquire an actual string, bow or body.
- **Direct prior art:** Gabriella Isaac, Lauren Hayes and Todd Ingalls, [Cross-Modal Terrains: Navigating Sonic Space through Haptic Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.00922), NIME 2017 (arXiv deposit 2020), §§3.1–3.3 and 4. Read the HTML methodology and conclusions. The authors describe successive force-feedback designs for a NovInt Falcon, including constant resistance during granulation, node-based terrain and procedural texture/force mappings. The first iteration's performer reports both greater attention to details and eventual predictability; the authors also report difficulty for audience members in understanding the performer's visible struggle (§3.1). Later mappings join force and sonic parameters (§3.3). Terrain and expressive resistance are therefore emphatically not new inventions here. The narrower proposed addition is an explicit *directed edge-cost score*, separate from vertex timbres, with a measurable circulation invariant. Their real force profiles are not assumed to be conservative or captured by this graph.
- **Epistemic tools:** Thor Magnusson, [Of Epistemic Tools: musical instruments as cognitive extensions](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1355771809000272), Organised Sound 14(2), 168–176 (2009), publisher abstract only. Magnusson argues that digital musical instruments can encapsulate musical knowledge and support thinking. Interpretation: encoding ease and difficulty may embody practical musical knowledge just as encoding a scale can embody a theoretical commitment. But a musician might use a route that the designer never intended; practical knowledge is not exhausted by the instrument's designer-authored map.
- **Negative empirical case:** Stefano Papetti, Hanna Järveläinen and Federico Fontana, [Design and Assessment of Digital Musical Devices Yielding Vibrotactile Feedback](https://doi.org/10.3390/arts12040143), Arts 12(4), 143 (2023), §§5–6, read the discussion and conclusions. They report perceived benefits of vibration while finding little demonstrable improvement in their particular performance tasks, and discuss task simplicity and expert participants as limitations. Vibrotactile feedback is not the same intervention as directional force resistance. The study nonetheless gives a reason not to equate an instrument's perceived liveliness with improved performance.
- **Accessibility as a counterposition:** Atau Tanaka and Adam Parkinson, [Haptic Wave: A Cross-Modal Interface for Visually Impaired Audio Producers](https://biglab.co.uk/publication/292/), CHI 2016, pp. 2150–2161, lab publication-page abstract only; DOI [10.1145/2858036.2858304](https://doi.org/10.1145/2858036.2858304). The abstract describes participatory development and real-world deployment of kinesthetic audio editing with visually impaired producers as experts. An additional modality can remove barriers rather than introduce obstacles. It would be a category mistake to identify difficulty with expressivity or ease with impoverishment.

These are bounded readings by GPT-6.2 using publicly available web pages on 2026-11-24, not systematic screening, independent reviewers, or a transfer of evidence into Track E/G. No source text is redistributed. The claims above describe what the authors report or argue, not universal laws. The strongest related design prior art is Isaac et al.; the distinction here is an exploratory design question, not a priority or patent claim.

## The exact finite construction

Use vertices 0,1,2,3 on a ring, a fixed sound at each vertex, and bidirectional edges. Clockwise edge i costs w_i in {0,...,8}, and its reverse costs 8-w_i. A cost is a declared abstract nonnegative integer, not joules, time, pain, exertion or perceived difficulty. Every paired there-and-back cost is 8; every instrument has the same unweighted graph and sound assignments.

The symmetric baseline of each edge is 4. Write a_i = w_i-4 for the clockwise directional residual; the reverse residual is -a_i. Define C=sum(a_i). The clockwise circuit costs 16+C and the anticlockwise circuit costs 16-C. Consequently their difference is 2C. If the field were representable by a scalar vertex potential U, then a_i=U(i+1)-U(i), and summing around the ring would give zero. Conversely if C=0, set U(0)=0 and U(i+1)=U(i)+a_i; the final closing edge is consistent. Thus C=0 iff the residual is a potential difference. The full cost, which includes the symmetric baseline, need not be path independent even then. C is not the net physical work of a real actuator.

Three instruments, same vertex presets and the same total paired cost:
 
| Field | Clockwise edge costs | Reverse edge costs (paired order) | CW / CCW circuit cost | C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level | 4,4,4,4 | 4,4,4,4 | 16 / 16 | 0 |
| Hills | 5,5,5,1 | 3,3,3,7 | 16 / 16 | 0 |
| Wind | 1,1,1,1 | 7,7,7,7 | 4 / 28 | -12 |

The hills have potential (0,1,2,3). The wind has no such potential. From vertex 0 to vertex 2, both two-step routes have cost 8 in Level; in Hills they cost 10 and 10; in Wind they cost 2 and 14. Neither a list of presets nor an undirected table of round-trip costs can distinguish them. Even a complete table of scalar vertex heights cannot encode Wind's directional residual.

### Exhaustive mechanical check

Run node oracle.cjs. Executed in V8 in this expedition: 6,561 integer fields, 489 admitting potentials, 6,072 not admitting potentials, 177,148 assertions passed. The oracle independently generates admissible potential vectors with U(0)=0 and compares their edge signatures against the entire cost census. For every field it also compares residuals for both simple routes between all twelve ordered distinct vertex pairs. The histogram is a count of stipulated equally enumerated cases, not a probability model for instruments in the world. Source was serialized from the executed function; no physical controller, listening trial or hardware test. No novelty claim for the elementary graph-gradient criterion.

A useful extension to arbitrary connected bidirectional graphs: remove the symmetric component of each edge cost. Choose a root and integrate the remaining antisymmetric residual along a spanning tree. Check each non-tree edge against its implied potential difference. The discrepancies are cycle residuals, not errors unless the designer specifically requires a potential. This provides a compact authoring diagnostic; the present oracle exhaustively verifies only the four-cycle.

## Composition rather than obstruction

The wind admits a musical direction without forbidding its reverse. It is not merely a mapping from gesture to sound; it is a mapping from a possible gesture to an anticipated cost of changing the sound. This gives another musician something to compose: the relative convenience of paths, the emergence of regions worth lingering in, and the moment a previously easy return becomes awkward. That second musician should not be silently given authority over the first musician's body.

In the companion [score](SCORE.md), costs become counters in an optional percussion lane. A cost unit can be one quiet click, rather than a forced delay or resisted movement. Both directions take the same number of beats in the fixed-time version. This makes a cost score legible while refusing to claim that clicks reproduce force feedback. An alternative tempo realization spends one additional unit of time per cost counter; it is a different artwork and should not be treated as a controlled comparison with the first.

A further experiment: give one performer a strong clockwise wind and another the opposite. Each experiences an independently configured virtual instrument. They can traverse the same nodes and end with the same note, but their cost accompaniment forms counterpoint. When they exchange fields, the topology does not change; only the distribution of inconvenience does. There is no requirement that musicians experience the stated cost as effort.

Counterargument: a frictionless instrument can support extremely intricate, skilled gestures; synthetic resistance may impede a performer's intentions rather than disclose a material. Difficulty can be an aesthetic resource but cannot serve as a universal proxy for authenticity, expertise or deservingness. A musician may choose to perform with no cost layer at all. There is no artistic obligation to justify that choice.

## Exaptation: a Weather Bus for synthesis

Separate the sound dictionary, transition graph, directed cost field, rendering modality and user override. A soft synthesizer may already have a preset morph graph; the Weather Bus attaches independent metadata to each directed edge. An author can paint symmetric costs (roughness), potential differences (hills), and antisymmetric cycle residues (wind). These should be distinct inspection layers, not three names for the same quantity. The graph can be changed without changing oscillator definitions. A route planner can expose minimum total cost, least peak cost and minimum steps separately, since they answer different questions.

A first software experiment uses an ordinary mouse, keyboard or accessible switch; it does not impose extra effort. Route previews show forward and reverse cost, and optionally sonify the per-step cost as a second voice. The performer can silence the voice, bypass costs or change input modality. An optional simulated-time mode uses declared time budgets. An author could patch the *signed cycle residual* into FM index, granular density or filter resonance, in addition to ordinary timbre morphing. With procedural agents, give each an independent cost field and compare route distributions while preserving the same destination set. Agent movement is an algorithmic result, not evidence of human intentionality.

If physical haptics are ever pursued, this graph is only a score-level abstraction. It does not specify actuator forces, stability, energy, comfort or safe operating limits. A separate controls/safety and accessibility review is required; the ability to disable or bypass force feedback must be independent of the artistic field. No hardware implementation is authorized by this expedition.

For MHAC, the transferable question concerns apparent reversibility: does undoing an operation require the same steps, time, attention, permissions or accessibility as doing it? A graph of state transitions alone cannot answer. For ordinary administrative interfaces, measure direction-specific cost dimensions rather than combine them into one invented physical measure. Design hypothesis: making route-specific undo costs visible can improve decisions about whether to act; compare directed previews, symmetric previews and no preview with fixed action outcomes in a future synthetic task. Measure comprehension, incorrect reversibility inference, successful recovery, time and burden; explicitly examine whether previews simply make the interface noisier. No operational warnings or required actions should be delayed for artistic effect.
