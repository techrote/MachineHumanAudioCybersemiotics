## An event label is not an event identity

The seven-witness predecessor [#809 / #810](https://github.com/techrote/MachineHumanAudioCybersemiotics/blob/cc2c89b39bb0f59593a586c4c2cafbd1a93ab4ed/research/expeditions/seven-witnesses/README.md) assumes each event occurs exactly once. Actual musical material repeats: a second A may be acoustically indistinguishable from a first A. Do overlapping witness accounts still determine a performance? What if one witness drops one note internally rather than disappearing as a whole? What if the note disappears upstream, before every witness?

The key distinction is between a type word (AABBCC), the physical identities of its occurrences, and a trace in which some order differences are deliberately irrelevant. Perfect type-word reconstruction does not prove recovery of independently assigned physical IDs. Given known multiplicities, rank-tagging A occurrences as A1,A2 in order is a canonical coordinate system, not externally authenticated identity. If an observer misses an A, rank-tagging their surviving As alone is unsafe.

## Sharp repeated-word correction theorem

Fix a finite type alphabet, strictly positive known multiplicity of each type, and all words realizing that multiset. Every monitor S reports the entire type word restricted to its subset of types, retaining all repetitions. Monitors are separate report coordinates, and monitor boundaries and erasure flags are trusted. A target trace is specified by a nonempty undirected graph G of relevant distinct-type pairs: two words have the same trace iff all their two-type projections on G agree. Same-type rank order is fixed by convention. Let c(ab) count monitors containing both a and b, and d be its minimum over G.

Why this is the trace notion: label the kth occurrence of each type by its rank; a two-type projection determines all cross-rank comparisons between those types. Form a DAG on ranked occurrences with edges for equal types and relevant distinct types, oriented by the word. Equal pair projections give the same DAG, whose topological orders are related by swaps of adjacent incomparable occurrences. Thus this agrees with the quotient by adjacent commutations of nonedges. Extra nonedge comparisons may be available to monitors, without being target requirements.

For two differing traces, some required two-type projection differs, and every monitor containing the pair must differ. A pair attaining d can be placed adjacently in a word and swapped. Only monitors containing that pair change, and each changed projection differs by precisely one adjacent unlike swap. So the minimum whole-report Hamming distance is d. Moreover, with fixed multiplicities, two unequal monitor words cannot be related by a single insertion, deletion or substitution: insert/delete changes length, substitution changes counts. Each differing report therefore has edit distance at least 2. The adjacent-swap witness achieves exactly 2 (delete then insert, or two substitutions), so the minimum *sum* of ordinary edit distances across report coordinates is 2d.

**Mixed-fault theorem.** Allow up to e arbitrary complete report replacements, f flagged complete report erasures, and E ordinary unit-cost symbol edits distributed across the remaining reports, per explanation. Universal target-trace identification holds iff

    2e + f + E < d.

Proof of sufficiency: two candidate explanations share the erasure mask, and at most 2e nonerased reports are replaced in one or both explanations. On remaining differing coordinates the total edit cost of both explanations is at least 2 per coordinate by the triangle inequality. At least max(0,d-2e-f) such coordinates remain. Total cost is at most 2E. This contradicts the strict inequality.

Proof of necessity: take the minimum-coverage adjacent-swap pair. Erase up to f differing coordinates; split up to 2e remaining differing coordinates into two replacement groups, at most e per explanation, choosing the other candidate's clean report on each. At each remaining differing coordinate delete the same one of the two swapped symbols in each prediction, giving a common word at cost one to each. If the inequality fails, the number of those coordinates is at most E. This gives a common received vector even if the symbol-edit channel permits deletions only. Zero coverage gives a clean collision. The result concerns a budget across reports, not a budget of upstream acoustic events.

If adjacent swaps are allowed as single primitive symbol operations, the exact analogous condition is 2e+f+2E<d. Each differing report then needs at least one edit in total, and the adjacent-swap witness permits distributing the remaining differing coordinates across the two explanations, each at most E. Calling transposition a single error changes the guarantee. A nominal "one error" is not a unit until its location and operation are specified.

## Candidate-list receiver and retrospective certificates

For a proposed word compute the ordinary Levenshtein distance to each nonerased report. Discard the e largest distances (or all if fewer exist); the remaining sum is the least permissible symbol-edit budget under arbitrary whole-report replacement. Accept the word if that sum is <=E, then group all accepted words by target trace. A list of traces rather than an arbitrary representative preserves ambiguity. The executable implementation enumerates the fixed-multiset words; complexity is factorial in total length in the worst case. Each comparison uses quadratic prefix dynamic programming and sorts report costs. This is an oracle, not an embedded realtime implementation.

For a fixed word p, its minimum whole-report distance to a different full type word is the minimum c(ab) over adjacent unlike symbols in p, because a different word reverses the order of some adjacent ranked occurrences; adjacent equal types cannot reverse their canonical ranks. Adjacent unlike swaps attain the bound, also giving twice that distance for the sum of ordinary edits. For a partial target graph the naive adjacent-relevant-type rule is insufficient: an indirectly observed relation may be forced through non-target symbols, or an alternative may require several swaps.

**Exact local trace certificate.** For any chosen erasure mask construct the ranked-occurrence DAG using same-type order and comparisons from surviving monitors. A different target trace is compatible iff there exists a required cross-type occurrence pair whose original forward ordering is absent from reachability. Indeed the surviving reports are exactly the topological orders of this DAG, after rank labels are removed. If a pair is incomparable, adding its reversed edge cannot create a cycle; a topological order with the reversed pair exists. If all required comparisons survive, every compatible word has the same trace. Minimizing erased monitor count among successful masks gives the exact fixed-word Hamming distance to a different target trace. This proof allows extra non-target monitors. The finite oracle enumerates erasure masks; no general polynomial minimum label-cut algorithm is claimed.

Concrete counterexample: p=AABBCC, target AC, monitors AB,AB,BC,BC. There are no AC monitors, hence universal distance zero (place A and C adjacent in some word). But for p, every A precedes every B and every B precedes every C. The target is forced by transitivity until both copies of either report type disappear. Fixed-word target distance 2, despite zero direct coverage. This is retrospective protection, not an acquisition guarantee.

## The same missing note at every witness

Suppose k occurrences of one known type a are deleted *upstream* before projection. All monitors then observe restrictions of one shared shorter word v. Give the receiver even the complete v and the original multiplicities: a stronger channel than any set of its projections.

Let m be the number of non-a occurrences in v. Its m+1 gaps around those fixed non-a symbols have known surviving a counts. Reinsert the k lost a occurrences by distributing k identical units among those gaps. Each weak composition produces a distinct original type word, and every possible original type word arises this way. Thus the exact number of compatible histories with a full surviving word is binomial(m+k,k). This includes m=0, when the original type word is already fixed. When m>0 a single shared deletion leaves m+1 distinct histories, and no number of identical downstream witnesses removes that ambiguity. Physical identities would create additional uncertainty not quantified here.

With two each of A,B,C, a single shared A deletion leaves five possibilities; deleting both As leaves fifteen. In contrast, five independent copies of each pair projection can universally correct any single local symbol edit, or a mix with e=1,f=1,E=1. For the same arrangement a single upstream deletion can affect ten report coordinates, because A occurs in both the AB and AC groups. The local budget must not be used as a physical common-cause fault budget.

This result connects to the earlier Lost Tick expedition: information lost before observation cannot be conjured through agreement of later observers. The analogy to archival testimony is conceptual: duplication does not imply independent witnessing. It does not establish intentional deception or a human-memory mechanism.

## Exaptation and negative sediment

A rehearsal monitor should report whether its failure was an entire lost report, a local dropped event, a substitution, a local swap or a suspected common upstream gap. A type-only receipt should not present occurrence rank as a durable physical identifier. A trace inspector could expose target comparisons, transitive certificates and missing comparisons separately, rather than requiring a global clock. A score can explicitly permit independent gestures to commute, but that is an authored choice of relevance; no causal claim follows from a stipulated graph. Conventional trace theory and coding theory explain the model; no incremental cybersemiotic mechanism or human benefit established.

Unknown or changing multiplicities, monitors seeing only selected occurrences of a type, unknown labels, timing/duration, shared channel failure and authenticated physical execution remain outside the correction theorem. An upstream gap can be undetectable if multiplicities are unknown. The exact finite decoder does not scale as implemented. The shared-deletion count assumes only one deleted type, no extra errors and full surviving word. No physical hardware, participant data, external publication or production plugin.
