## Question and contract

The earlier coverage oracle permits arbitrary local omissions and cannot infer upstream loss from invisibility. Now suppose every event enters a known rooted distribution tree, with one incoming edge at the root, and every leaf is a labeled witness in scope for every event. On each event, a cut of an edge suppresses that occurrence for all its descendants, with no recovery, insertion, substitution, delay or cross-tree leakage. Cuts may vary arbitrarily per event. Count only the first effective cuts: descendants of a cut cannot contribute additional effective cuts for that event. Tree nodes have at least two children unless leaves, and the trees are finite. Reports retain order but not occurrence IDs. Unit cut count is a declared parsimony objective, NOT physical fault probability, persistence, or causal evidence. A cut may stand for a local omission at a leaf. No claim about arbitrary DAGs or type-specific structural blind spots.

## Fixed occurrence: the unique coarsest explanation

For a tree node v let L(v) be its descendant leaf set. If S is the set of witnesses that actually retain one occurrence, an effective cut set is an antichain of nodes whose leaf sets partition the complement of S. All missing leaves are accounted for, no seen leaf can be cut, and nested cuts are redundant by definition. Let F(S) be the collection of maximal all-missing clades (including the root when S is empty).

The unique minimum-cardinality effective cut set is F(S). Proof: every valid cut lies inside precisely one maximal missing clade. Each such clade requires at least one cut. A single cut covering that clade must be its own incoming edge (no unary vertices); the maximal clades partition the absent leaves. Thus the coarsest set is uniquely optimal. Every other antichain explanation is obtained by repeatedly replacing a chosen internal node by all its children. Conversely every such refinement is valid; prove recursively inside each maximal clade. The maximum number of effective cuts is the number of absent leaves, obtained by cutting only leaf edges.

An exact additive identity follows: the minimum is the number of missing leaf incidences minus the sum of (degree(v)-1) over all internal nodes whose entire descendant leaf set is missing. Induction: if a whole subtree is missing, its leaf count minus its total internal degree excess is one; otherwise apply the identity to child subtrees. This gives an alternative score computation and exposes the contribution of each shared fault domain.

## The arity spectrum theorem

If every internal node has d children, d >= 2, each refinement increases the cut count by precisely d-1. Start at the coarsest explanation and refine to leaf-only cuts, visiting every term in that arithmetic progression. All valid cuts arise by refinements, hence no other counts occur. If C is the minimum and M the number of missing leaves, the exact support is C, C+(d-1), ..., M.

For binary trees every integer from C through M is feasible. Conversely, among trees without unary vertices, if every possible leaf visibility pattern has an interval of feasible cut counts, the tree must be binary: for any node with degree d>2 make precisely its clade invisible. The first explanation has one cut and the smallest distinct refinement has d cuts, so counts 2 through d-1 are absent. A trifurcation and its binary refinement have different spectra for identical evidence. This is not evidence that the physical split is binary; inserting hypothetical relay nodes alters the admissible causes.

## Repeated symbols: exact joint polynomial and preserved spectrum

For a known source word w of length N and h leaf reports, scan source occurrences. At position t choose any subset S of witnesses whose next desired symbol matches w[t], advance precisely those report prefixes. For the chosen visibility set use a polynomial with variables z for effective cut count, x for completely invisible occurrence count, and y for root-edge cut count. The per-node recurrence is the product of child polynomials, plus a monomial z when the entire clade is unseen; at an unseen leaf the factor is z, otherwise 1. At the root the extra root-cut alternative is zy rather than z, and multiply the entire polynomial by x if no witness sees the event. The product option means no cut at that node; the added option means cut there, no descendant cuts. Distinct terms count labeled antichain choices, not hidden redundant cuts. Across source events multiply factors and sum over all compatible prefix paths; accept only fully consumed reports. Each legal embedding tuple and effective cut tuple gives exactly one term contribution, so this recurrence counts them exactly. No probabilistic interpretation is imposed.

The maximum cut count for EVERY feasible alignment is the same M_total = h*N - sum(report lengths). The minimum depends on alignment and topology. For a full d-ary tree the cut spectrum for each alignment is a progression ending at this common maximum with step d-1. Since the progression containing the global minimum contains all other progressions, the exact global support, even with latent alignments, is the progression from the global minimum to M_total. The proof also works when source order is unknown but length fixed and all feasible source words are allowed: each feasible word/alignment has the same M_total. The implemented oracle covers known source only. An empty support means inconsistency, not zero faults.

For fixed alignment the minimum number of root cuts under an at-most budget B >= C is max(0,U-floor((B-C)/(d-1))), where U counts completely invisible occurrences and C is the coarsest total. The coarsest cut tuple uses U root cuts; each root cut removed costs at least one refinement (d-1), and refining just the root to its d children achieves this cost independently. Maximum root cuts under the same budget is U. This requires a nontrivial full d-ary tree. To extend across latent alignments, take extrema over feasible alignments with C <= B. Without a budget, root cuts can always be zero; parsimony cannot authenticate a physical cause.
