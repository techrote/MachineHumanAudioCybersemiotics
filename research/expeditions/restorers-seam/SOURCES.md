Counterargument: perception may complete fragments to useful ends and archival access may legitimately be enhanced. The contention is about presenting generated data as acquired data, not the morality of continuity. An independent recording could establish the missing notes; a valid restrictive grammar could make the admissible set singleton. Revision criterion: evidence of a model violation or independently warranted completion changes the applicable inference, while the acquisition-vs-derivation distinction remains.
