# Score for a sound that acquires a verb

Original performance instructions, not a psychology experiment. No outcome is required. The generated sounds are optional materials. Play at a comfortable level; any listener may stop or decline. If an unprimed encounter is desired, hear 01-target.wav before reading further or opening render.py.

## I. A word without a witness

Play the isolated four-band target once. Wait. Permit descriptions of the sound that contain no nouns for its source. Play it again. Permit nouns, but do not require any. No answer is announced. The silence between repetitions belongs to the piece.

## II. Two curators and one object

The first curator offers the sentence "The glass remembers the rain." The second offers "The grass remembers the train." Neither may edit the target. Their cue can be given in writing, spoken by the performer, or as the clear speech segment of the supplied montage. Use 03, 04 and 05 for neutral, matching and nonmatching lead-ins; they all finish with the same target. The audience is invited to notice whether its own descriptions change. If nothing changes, do not replay until something does. End the movement anyway.

If performed in two rooms, the curators exchange their roles but not the audience. Their second performances are not to be advertised as fresh first encounters.

## III. The percussionist who splits

The three tone files contain one isolated ABA sequence and two versions with different pitch-separation histories. Play 06, then either 07 or 08, then stop. One performer taps a composite galloping rhythm; another attends to one pitch strand. Either may change what they attend to without changing the source. They may disagree. A third performer can choose to play an unrelated sound during the interval or leave it silent; no reset of experience is promised.

## IV. An archive with a missing field

Record the order in which sounds and cues were offered, and the paths to their unchanged target files. Do not record an audience's imagined reactions. Invite the audience to leave with its own account, or none. If the work is performed again, label the occasion REVISIT rather than RESET.

## Fiction: The Department of First Hearings

The last phonograph in the city had only one recording. Its curator could not understand why the oldest visitors insisted it was a bird and the children maintained it was a sentence. A technician made eight thousand copies and established that every sample was the same.

The Department commissioned a switch that would restore a visitor to the moment before the caption was read. The switch was delivered in a brass housing and worked perfectly on the caption. The Department discovered the error during the opening ceremony.

The curator was allowed to cover the label but not to take away the visitor's evening.

After the museum closed, a sound engineer rearranged the archive. She kept the recording in one drawer and all the conditions under which it had been offered in another. The third drawer was empty. Its little plaque said: THE THING HEARD.

A visitor asked if anything was missing. The curator replied that there was no recording of the answer.

The sound was played once more, and for the first time the museum did not insist that the visitor hear it for the first time.
