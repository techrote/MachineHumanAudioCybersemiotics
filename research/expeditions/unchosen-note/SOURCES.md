## Adversarial reading

The most direct counterposition is a completely specified deterministic transition system: in this one-step model a single actual menu has equal union and intersection. Thus the failure of trace-level inference depends on the family of menus and on what the observations exclude. A complete action-intervention trace including refusals can distinguish cases that the successful-note language cannot.

Gaver's relational account also resists identifying affordances with abstract menu entries: actor and environment matter. Cage's artistic practice resists the assumption that maximizing immediate choice is the composer's goal. Neither source endorses the proposed instrument or a normative freedom metric. No participant data, perceptual proof, endorsement, copied audio or historical score is used.
