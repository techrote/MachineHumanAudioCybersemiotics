# Novelty and overlap matrix — MHAC-R002

[Novelty index](README.md) · [Nees–Liebman record](COMPARATOR_NEES_LIEBMAN_2023.md) · [CSV](OVERLAP_MATRIX.csv) · [Protocol](../protocol/PROTOCOL-v1.md)

**Novelty is treated as an open question.** Each cell states what an inspected comparator appears to cover, how much of it was actually read, and what would overturn the judgement. "Not found" always means *not found in the material inspected here*; it never asserts a literature-wide gap. The audit is bounded to comparators reachable on 2026-10-13 without paid access, new accounts or contact. Source records: [`research/novelty/records/`](records/).

## Overlap status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `covered` | An inspected comparator addresses this question with comparable scope; MHAC work would be replication or update. |
| `partly_covered` | Components are addressed somewhere, but the MHAC question's combination or unit of analysis was not found. |
| `not_found_inspected` | No inspected comparator addresses it. Inspection extent is stated; wider searches may find it. |
| `unknown` | The relevant comparator was identified only by title/metadata or not identified; no judgement possible. |

## Conditional conclusion

Read together, the inspected comparators make a broad cue-family empirical review redundant (N01) and leave open, at most, (a) an evidence map organised by message semantics, action, training and retention rather than by cue family (N02, N03, N06), (b) an honest update window (N13), and (c) a critical test of whether cybersemiotics changes anything relative to human-factors and semiotic-engineering accounts (N09, N10, N12). Each remains a question. After R004, N04 and N07 are partly covered by identified comparators, and N09's core texts remain unread. These three still carry the highest risk of hidden redundancy. The [protocol](../protocol/PROTOCOL-v1.md#novelty-and-scope-consequences) converts these consequences into scope rules.

## Limits

One agent performed this audit with web search and a summarising fetch tool. There was no independent human check, no subscription database search and no VoR for C1. Abstract-only comparators (C4, C8, C9, part of C6) support only the cell statements above. MHAC-R004 re-judged N04 and N06–N09 and extended N12 on 2026-10-13, mostly from abstracts and a few read passages (C11–C16). It must be rerun again when those comparators are read in full and after R005 lands.
