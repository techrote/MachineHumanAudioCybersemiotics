## Why this is needed

Close reading with exact page locators needs the actual files, so their bytes can be hashed and pages cited. The agent's workspace cannot download from publishers or repositories, and its web reader returns only paraphrase. Without files, #7 can make only bounded, explicitly tool-mediated statements, and no whole-book claim about the 2008 monograph.

## How files are handled

The agent hashes each file, records its SHA-256 and page extent, and reads it with page locators. Files stay in your folder; only openly licensed files (CC BY) could ever be copied into the public repository, and only if you say so. Nothing is purchased, no account is created and nobody is contacted on your behalf.
