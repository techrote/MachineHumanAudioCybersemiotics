# Decision memo — MHAC-T006, 2026-11-03 UTC

**Current classification: provisional methodological tool, not an accepted empirical finding or design rule.** 47 declared-fiction symbolic tests exercise ordinary truthful direct commands, prohibitions, corrections and factual states alongside missing NOT, scope inversion, forbidden action, stale source versions, expiry, unknown authorization, silence and false all-clear. An accepted case means only that the invented proposition is consistent with the invented truth. Count of test cases is not a human accuracy rate.

**Key falsification:** `prohibit(A)` does not generally imply `require(B)`; `not cleared` is a factual negative, not an instruction to refrain; an acknowledgment or missing alarm is not a resolved hazard. `clearance_unconfirmed` remains **unresolved** because the provisional authority lacks proof-of-clearance records. These intentional limits prevent artificial positive conclusions.

| Future result | Decision implication, not automatic policy |
