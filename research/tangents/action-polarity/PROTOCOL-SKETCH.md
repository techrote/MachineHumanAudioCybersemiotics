# Candidate R014 protocol (not preregistered, approved or executed)

**Question:** for truthful spoken action instructions, how do operator scope and prior expectation interact with deadline pressure and masking to affect *first and final consequential action*? This proposed study is NOT an experiment in this PR.

## Proposed design (conditional on R014/R020 human readiness)

- Independently establish source/event/version and true action policy. Distinguish: B required + A forbidden; only A forbidden while B permitted; known active; known inactive; and unknown. Randomize *only truthful* renderings within each stratum; impossible equivalents are **excluded and reported**, not fabricated.
- Cross candidate operator (positive direct, prohibition, truthful contrastive, separate factual report), prior expectation (A-prepotent/neutral), deadline (tight/loose), background speech/noise, negator placement and message length. Counterbalance order/training. Use explicit source/version-bound accessible non-audio/repeat baseline. Predefine effects of unequal time-on-air, language, hearing/assistive devices, accent and visual workload.
- Log **distinct endpoints**: message detected; target action interpreted; first attempted action; forbidden first action; corrections/reversals; final correct action before cutoff; time-to-final-correct-action with censored omissions; false all-clear; subjective effort/urgency; interrupted-task cost. A fast wrong action is not a successful response.
- Establish analysis strata and feasibility, with uncertainty/interaction bounds, missingness and potential competing events; do not infer equivalence from nonsignificance. No effect sizes, participant dataset or invented sample size is supplied.
- R020 requires genuine authorization, safe listening/ethics and consent/privacy/accessibility safeguards before any human procedure. No actual spoken cues, participant contact or hardware are executed by this tangent. Source screening remains R005/R006; R008/R011/R012–R014 control adoption.
