- No full restricted Wheale articles were accessed. Their experiments **may overlap**; R006 must resolve publication–study–sample identity before any counting or pooling.
- Oxford identifies an accepted S60 manuscript with its own terms; its existence is not a claim that the text was read. S61 and S62 open HTML were consulted only in the stated ranges.
- The S62 correction occurred after the frozen cutoff and is noted as **versioned source currency**, not retrospective screening.
- A provisional symbolic grammar is a **methodological tool**; any benefit from spoken polarity is a **hypothesis**. Neither is an empirical MHAC finding. R005/R006 intake, risk-of-bias and exact locators are separate authorized tasks.
