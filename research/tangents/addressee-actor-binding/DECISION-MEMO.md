# MHAC-T008 decision memo — 2026-11-03

**Decision:** retain addressee versus authorized actor as a **provisional falsifiable distinction**, but **do not adopt an acoustic recipient grammar or security model**. The synthetic-only comparison exposed wrong-actor acceptance by a content-only policy, while the strong external-authorization comparator yielded no false accepts on this hand-selected fixture set. A confirmation requirement caused many false rejections. These results narrow, rather than prove, the case for adding acoustic complexity.

## Interpretation matrix

| Observation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Content-correct message permits wrong actor in source-only model | Preserve independent permission/audience construct as a candidate design check |
| External-role broadcast baseline performs as well/better | Prefer external control and no additional acoustic grammar unless other evidence justifies it |
| Target/confirmation model rejects correct authorized attempts | Study deadline and unnecessary verification costs; don't manufacture a positive result |
| Address recognition, permission or delivery cannot be established | Explicitly classify as unobserved, not as authorized action |
| Real deployment has no actor-specific actions | **Do not pursue** this scope extension |
| Future human data contradict synthetic advantages | Prefer empirical action/response evidence to synthetic policy counts |

## Unresolved and limitations

1. Perfect access to `authorized_actors` and current role version is assumed. No authentication, authorization updates, identity verification or real task ownership is implemented.
2. Near-homophone, clipped, absent and competing messages are **declared labels**, not manipulated waveforms, hearing tests or measured errors.
3. Source families include human–human radio incidents and conversation. The frozen Track E E2 rule excludes these from direct empirical eligibility. R005/R006 must judge publication–study–sample identity and overlap; particularly Monan/Cardosi ASRS populations and Wood/Conway research lineage.
4. This is a fixed tiny hand-selected fictional grid; deterministic model rankings do not validate generalizability, salience, fairness, feasibility or safety.
5. Source abstracts and official catalogues were consulted, not all complete primary papers. No unverified source statistics were imported as MHAC action outcomes.
6. Formal design adoption depends on R008/R011 synthesis, R012/R013 readiness and human R020 gate for participant work.
