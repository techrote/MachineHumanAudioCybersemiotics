# Conditional R014 human-study handoff — **not executed**

**Gate:** R005/R006 source adjudication and programme dependencies must precede design adoption; actual listening/recruitment/data collection needs explicit R020 authorization, appropriate ethics/consent and accessibility provisions. Nothing in this issue authorizes exposure or creates a real-participant record.

## Proposed independent factors

Message representation: broadcast/external assignment, explicit addressee, addressee plus actionable readback and accessible non-audio fallback. Cross controlled listener role (target/authorized, bystander/not authorized, group target, changed or unknown assignment), conflicting message timing, lexical similarity, truncation, concurrent workload and address presentation. Hold source/event truth independent of the cue. Avoid real person names, voice imitation, operational control or unsafe instructions.

## Endpoints and instrumentation

Record **first** actor to attempt action, authorized-vs-wrong actor under independently supplied roles, final correction, omitted action, missed deadline, acknowledgement identity/version, correct address recognition, source/event comprehension, subjective workload, and concurrent-task interference separately. Add manipulation checks for actual audibility, intelligibility and permission comprehension, rather than treating a recognized name or correct recall as evidence of authorized action.

Use a randomized/counterbalanced, safely calibrated design **only after authorization**, with declared attrition, error tolerance and prospective analyses. Stratify or record hearing/assistive devices, language/accent, communication conventions, room/multiple-listener layout, task training and role-change familiarity; offer non-audio accessibility and opt-out. Do not prescribe an age- or hearing-specific acoustic scheme from scouting abstracts.

## Falsifying outcomes

If broadcast-plus-external permission is non-inferior under tested conditions, do not prescribe an acoustic call-sign grammar. If addressing improves only recognition but not wrong-actor actions or deadline performance, narrow claims. If confirmations increase correct actions but produce missed deadlines, report a trade-off. If attribution is unresolved, report unknown, not success. No result of the present synthetic oracle predicts human effect magnitude.
