**Frozen protocol boundary:** Track E E2 excludes human-to-human speech studies; discovering pre-cutoff studies does not mean inclusion. All six originate prior to frozen 2026-10-04 search cutoff. R005/R006 governs formal screening, duplicated publication/study/sample identities, source rights and full text. Track G theory appraisal has different criteria. Nothing from these abstracts is promoted to a selected empirical effect, human-action metric or accepted MHAC finding.

**Deduplication:** S102/S107 both use aviation voluntary-report contexts (potential unknown incident overlap). S105/S106 share experimental authors and theoretical lineage (sample independence unverified). S104's addressee benefit includes interactive coordination, so it cannot validate a single acoustic-recipient label. All negative/zero/no-added-value outcomes are retained.
