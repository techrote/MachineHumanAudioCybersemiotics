## Positive structural counterexample

The deliberately weak proximity and source-device policies falsely assert independence (27 and 19 cases) and lose action obligations (4 and 6) within the authored cases. A device is not a reading and neither timing nor cue plurality proves causal independence. This warrants a candidate **review question**, not a requirement: should a future R012 semantic model expose evidence lineage separately from source device and world truth, and should R013 prohibit treating displayed/voiced plurality as source corroboration *unless provenance is externally justified*?

## Null, contradiction and adoption conditions

**Null observed in this fictional suite:** the nonaggregating D baseline has zero false independent-evidence assertions and zero missed required obligations, with fewer proxy tokens than lineage-aware C. Therefore **do not mandate a lineage-fusion subsystem** based on this comparison. A minimal no-inferred-corroboration / no-cross-event suppression rule may be simpler.

**Contradiction/cost:** C suppresses four duplicated offers, but paying extra annotation words without demonstrated interpretive benefit could cause crowding or deadline costs; in auditory streams, multiple reports may improve detection even if from one observation. A classifier with falsely asserted "verified independent" metadata could be worse than D; this oracle assumes the metadata is true and cannot falsify upstream provenance.

**Inconclusive:** original full studies not comprehensively reviewed, no selected empirical MHAC source corpus, no real listener input or acoustic manipulation; the theories offer different possible interpretations and the study may not be applicable when no concurrent warnings exist. Formal R005/R006 screening and R020 gate remain unresolved.
