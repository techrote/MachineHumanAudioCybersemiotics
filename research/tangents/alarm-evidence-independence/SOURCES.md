**Current classification.** Source-reported within-task observations are established only within their described study settings; security correlation and auditory streaming are modelling tools/analogies; the conjunction 'two sounds → two independent machine observations' is an **untested MHAC hypothesis**, not a measured effect. A strict nonaggregating message policy is the principal countermodel. Scholarly overlap is unresolved and seven entries are **not seven independent empirical effects**. No source warrants a compulsory extra audio field, sensor inference service or anthropomorphic voice strategy.

**Identity caution.** The optional synthetic-voice item S281 has conflicting publisher authorship metadata reported in scout #58 and is intentionally **excluded**. No multi-voice interface requirement was found in accepted main. Do not use it to inflate this source list or claim evidence of voice-induced false corroboration.
