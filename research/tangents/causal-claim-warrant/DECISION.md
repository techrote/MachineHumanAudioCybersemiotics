## Precommitted interpretations

- **Positive logical falsification:** any A case asserts cause without discriminating warrant, or offers an independently unwarranted remedy. This only rejects the *naive inference* in fictional cases; it does not validate D as an auditory policy.
- **Null / simpler comparator:** B is allowed to tie D on false causal certainty and invalid action offers with less narration; B's omissions are reported separately, not scored as dangerous without human/task evidence.
- **Counterevidence:** C may surface hypotheses useful for later diagnosis even without asserting one. Müller et al. (2020) reported efficient visual diagnostic checking with multiple alternatives, and mostly active checking of single favored diagnoses. A blanket explanation ban is not warranted.
- **Negative tradeoff:** D's evidence gating or C's candidate enumeration may exceed a constructed word budget; that is not observed delay and may justify no additional speech.
- **Inconclusive:** formal source selection, diagnostic-test validity and listening/behavioral outcomes remain unresolved. Record no MHAC user-effectiveness or theoretical advantage.
