# MHAC-T012 — provisional decision and human-gated empirical sketch

**Decision (conditional):** A longitudinal mapping *collision* under two permitted codebooks is a potential structural hazard for a persistent learned-cue product. Versioned manifests are a candidate safeguard *only where codebook migration/coexistence exists*. External current-state truth may suffice instead; the correct requirement may be **no learned cue migration**. An acoustic redesign may improve comfort/recognition in some settings, so freezing legacy sound assets forever is not justified by symbolic tests.

## Outcomes and reversible consequences

| Possible outcome | Interpretation and next action |
|---|---|
| Positive structural cross-version conflict | Propose a minimal optional `cue_asset_id`, generation manifest/version, interpretation binding and unknown-state fallback for later R012 review, not immediate adoption. |
| Null: compatible aliases / no learned code / truthful external legend | Recommend no additional audio-codebook machinery; preserve low-complexity baseline, do not create a subsystem. |
| Contradictory: legacy freeze fails while redesigned icons/voice are better | Treat redesign benefit and migration cost as separate empirical questions; do not select a sound family from this oracle. |
| Inconclusive: unverifiable version/source/clock/authority | Abstain from asserting migration correctness; record missing authoritative input, keep R013 contract open. |

## Prospective empirical protocol (not performed)

Counterbalance prior training (`v1`/`v2`) × acoustic change (unchanged/retuned) × semantic remap (none/referent/action/priority) × coexistence (single version/mixed versions/rollback), with matched exposure durations and training checks. Include a verifiably truthful external visual/text authority comparator and a silence/abstention condition. **Measure separately**: cue detection, source and critical-field interpretation, priority, first *intended* action, valid action-by-deadline, late correction, retention after delay, perceived burden/annoyance, and unresolved/unknown responses. Avoid equating reported recognition with safe action.

Before any human task: R020 authorization, accepted R014 readiness, ethics/consent/privacy and safe listening, balanced samples including hearing variation/hearing aids, language/accent, familiarity, age/accessibility and dual-task workload. No fabricated participants, no sample size/power justification without pilot variance, and no external outreach or deployment from this PR.

## Prepublication rejection checks

Reject a production requirement if no genuine cross-version learned cue use case, if all permitted live mappings remain equivalent, if canonical nonaudio state supersedes sound entirely, if true version cannot be attested at decision time, or if migration costs exceed any hypothesized benefit. Symbolic policy results, regardless of magnitude, are not empirical effect sizes.
