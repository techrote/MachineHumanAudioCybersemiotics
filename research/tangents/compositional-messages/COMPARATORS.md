# Alternative mechanism matrix — no human performance scores

| Mechanism | Inventory and held-out ability *by construction* | Duration / field binding risk | Training / congruence / access risks | Correct-action/deadline implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Flat memorised motif** | One independent whole-pair token per trained message; untrained legal pairs have no token by default. | Short token possible; source/event cannot be independently recovered by the syntax alone. | Grows with catalogue; needs pairing practice, delayed retention checks and non-audio alternatives. | Known motifs could be fast but novel pairs have no guaranteed decoding; make it a genuine baseline. |
| **Serial composition** | Reuses three source + three event tokens; three trained and three held-out legal combinations in toy grammar. | Fixed field order supports symbolic parse but longer audible duration and order-related memory interference are possible. | Training needs role/order awareness; both cues must survive masking, hearing variations, and transfer beyond hierarchy navigation. | Test *complete action*, omission and time-to-action, not just component name recall. |
| **Parallel composition** | Same theoretical inventory as serial; tag-dependent simultaneous fields. | Shorter on-air time possible, but stream segregation, timbre interference and **misbinding** may offset savings. Symbolic \`tagged\` cannot guarantee a perceptual tag. | Test cue overlap, attention split, spectral composition and assistive-device transformations; do not assume equal scores. | Prefer only if field-accurate action and interference are non-inferior within justified margins. |
| **Speech/accessible hybrid** | Explicitly names source/event with optional learned shorthand; may support unseen word combinations. | More tokens/speech duration, intelligibility/noise/language burden; optional visual/text confirmation and clarification. | Speech comprehension, multilingual access, hearing variation, task switches, prosodic urgency and privacy must be controlled. | Baseline/fallback contender; audio compositional “efficiency” must beat meaningful error, time and action trade-offs. |

**Source basis and limits:** S9/S10 abstract reports concern menu hierarchy recall; S11 serial/parallel comparison reports recognition, not cross-field source/event action under concurrent alarms; S12 congruence/training penalties are a warning against assuming arbitrary cues generalise. See [SOURCE-IDENTITIES.md](SOURCE-IDENTITIES.md) for bibliographic details, access extent and unknown sample overlap. No rows above are empirically ranked for MHAC.

**Negative controls:** match the same source/event payload and permissible actions; balance message frequency, prior exposure, vocabulary size, codebook complexity, noise, duration/loudness, training type, delayed recall, hearing profiles, and source×event frequencies. Distinguish a formally held-out *combination* from a held-out *unit* or an impossible combination. No simulation here can estimate listener confusion or hazard safety.
