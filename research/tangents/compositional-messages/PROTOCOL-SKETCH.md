## Aim, design and failure criteria

Four conditions: (A) flat learned motif baseline, (B) serial compounds, (C) parallel compounds, (D) clear speech plus optional non-audio confirmation. Within each condition, identify source and event separately, then decide the specified *fictional* task action. Cross factorial **trained vs legal held-out source×event pair**, **low vs higher secondary-task load**, **quiet vs challenging listening condition**; referent congruence and trained dose are either balanced or explicitly manipulated in a separately powered contrast. Randomise trial order and counterbalance condition mapping and sequence with participant-level assignment. Keep illegal/unknown input probes segregated from meaningful paired actions.

**Primary estimand proposal:** difference between mechanisms in probability of *fully correct (source,event,action) before a prespecified fictional deadline*, over all assigned trials (including no responses and omissions, not only responders). Compare held-out minus trained difference-in-differences as generalisation cost. Prespecify task-derived meaningful difference/non-inferiority margin with domain experts **before** collecting outcomes; do not fabricate one. If endpoint is poorly defined or samples inadequate, declare inconclusive.

Secondary endpoints: component token recognition, field binding (swapped source/event or wrong source), whole-message interpretation, source assignment with concurrent **independent** alarms, request-action selection, time-to-correct action including censored/no-action trials, perceived urgency, irrelevant-task errors, annoyance/workload, training trial count, delayed retention, accessibility/fallback use. Report all denominators and missingness; do not claim a fast response among survivors offsets omitted actions.

## Controls and falsifiers

- Same legal source/event/action information payload and the same opportunities for confirmation/repeat across conditions.
- Explicitly log signal energy, level, spectral content, physical duration, order, channel mapping and playback chain; duration itself may be a mechanism and should be analysed, not erased by manipulating loudness.
- Balance voice language, prosody, speech rate, textual fallback and intelligibility; test hearing-assistive and headphone transformations prospectively rather than inventing “universal” listener profiles.
- Standardised practice with a fixed training budget; delayed test without re-exposure; assess music experience, prior earcon exposure, language, hearing differences and cultural congruence without inferring sensitive identities.
- Incorrect field binding and failed action are *distinct* outcomes. A parallel signal may reduce time-on-air while increasing misbinding. Synthetic correctness does not constrain either.
- Include flat and speech baselines that could win; include no-benefit, worse-attention and different-by-task alternatives; predefine multiplicity and participant/sample dependence before inference.

## Hypotheses / decision tests

- **H1 (generalisation):** held-out legal pair binding suffers no meaningful larger penalty with a compositional mechanism than with an adequately trained flat/speech comparator. Countercase: considerably more held-out field swaps, incomplete actions or missed deadlines.
- **H2 (parallelisation):** parallel composition reduces signal duration without compromising timely *correct-action* probability; countercase: stream competition raises omission/field errors despite shorter playback.
- **H3 (training/congruence):** more exposure does not automatically eliminate all abstract or incongruent warning disadvantages. Countercase: differences converge after matched training and delayed retention.
- **H4 (scoped semantics):** some fields may be best left out of acoustic composition and communicated explicitly as needed; countercase: expanded grammar shows independently appraised advantages without unacceptable confounds.

Estimate uncertainty intervals and handle repeated observations with participant/item crossed effects or justified alternatives. Avoid post hoc adjusting of meaningful margins based on results; document ambiguous/undecodable trials rather than silently dropping them. A human-powered sample size cannot be derived from the 24 fictional oracle cases. No human outcome was produced here.
