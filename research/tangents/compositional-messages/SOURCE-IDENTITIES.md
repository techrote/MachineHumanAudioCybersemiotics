## Counterposition and consequence

Human recognition of 27 menu-node codes following training cannot demonstrate correct field identification, source assignment, urgent action or across-domain generalisation. S12 suggests congruence/practice are consequential, potentially favouring speech or a smaller conventional code. Opposite/null results are informative: a concise compositional grammar may be technically neat and operationally worse. **Classification:** S9–S12 source-reported abstract claims; MHAC mapping = plausible analogy/hypothesis, S12 = source-reported counterexample, this file = methodological/source-identity aid, no accepted empirical finding.
