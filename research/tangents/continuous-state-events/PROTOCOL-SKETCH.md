# MHAC-T005 — Prospective, unexecuted human experiment design

**Proposal only**, subject to R013/R014 derivation from accepted evidence and **R020 explicit human/ethics authorization**. No acoustic implementation, participant recruitment, personal data, sound exposure, registration or pilot result exists.

## Falsifiable design questions and hypotheses

- **H1 (dual-task trade-off):** against discrete alert + inspectable visual status, persistent status audio improves source trend/threshold monitoring *without* reducing correct first action on a separate time-critical event. A missed-event or slower-action penalty invalidates broad superiority even if trend recognition improves.
- **H2 (explicit change):** a short explicit boundary/clear indication improves accurate *source-state* classification relative to interpreting background disappearance as recovery. Countercase: temporal regularity or visual confirmation makes the explicit cue redundant.
- **H3 (hybrid prioritization):** event-time ducking of persistent audio improves separate-alert detection at comparable intelligibility and exposure relative to uninterrupted persistent+event mixing. Countercase: ducking is itself misinterpreted as source clearing, or abrupt cue changes increase error.
- **H4 (simpler alternative):** discrete-only+visual status may achieve equal/better timely action with lower auditory burden; non-significant differences alone do not establish equivalence or noninferiority.

## Candidate conditions and controls

A within-participant counterbalanced design *if later approved*: (A) discrete warning+visual status; (B) persistent status+discrete warning; (C) persistent status+explicit transition and clear; (D) persistent status+ducked time-critical warning. Optional E persistent-only and F structured visual-only controls require a justified extra workload and separation of learning order. Each condition receives matched state trajectories, sources, event identities, intended actions, event frequencies/deadlines and visual access, with scenario difficulty counterbalanced. Train sound mappings comparably and test delayed recognition where warranted.

Specify and instrument **A-weighted exposure, event-level sound energy, peak levels, spectral masking, duration/time-on-air, gain, playback equipment, ambient noise, hearing correction and relative loudness**. Do not pretend all conditions can have identical sound time if persistent audio is inherently more continuous; measure exposure as a mediator and report it rather than covertly normalizing away the treatment. Also vary scene regularity, 1-vs-multiple-source load, visual focus, background speech, and primary task demand in bounded strata—not every combination in a small pilot.

## Prespecified outcomes, not proxy equivalences

**Primary consequential outcome:** correctly chosen first action completed before a defined scenario deadline; code wrong action, no action, late action and censoring separately. **Companion safety outcomes:** false all-clear acceptance, missed event, event-source misattribution, failure to notice disappearance/recovery, and confirmation of unresolved source. **Secondary outcomes:** state trend/range classification, threshold transition identification, auditory event recognition, subjective workload/discomfort, training retention, visual primary-task accuracy/RT and interruptions. Track acoustic event offer and participant detection separately.

Decide a meaningful risk margin or noninferiority bound before a real study, and disclose multiplicity, learning/carryover, possible ceiling/floor effects and missing responses. A power/precision analysis must use justified assumptions, not the percentages of synthetic oracle fixtures. Analyze listener-level repeated measures with task/condition effects and confidence intervals; stratify by hearing/accessibility and prior sonification experience where sample permits. Do not use a non-significant p-value as evidence of equal performance.

Real listener exposure requires #20's actual authorization, ethics/consent/privacy/record-handling arrangements, sound-level safeguards, accessible non-auditory alternatives and independent oversight as appropriate. Historical sources require R005/R006 study/sample identity, selection and R009 appraisal before empirical claims feed R013. If observed masking or false clear increases in a future authorized test, reject/narrow the candidate even when trend comprehension improves. If the model cannot identify reliable source recovery, do not publish clear based on sound cessation.
