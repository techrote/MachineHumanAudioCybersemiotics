**Evidence relationship:** S30 and S31 evaluate different tasks, populations and designs and should not be pooled naively; S32 is clinically themed but uses a nonclinician laboratory task; S34/S35 investigate scene perception rather than verified corrective action. Review-study and source identity overlap are unresolved until R005/R006; no made-up publication, study, sample or independent effect counts are inferred. All source findings above are *reported within their own tasks*, separate from the *hypothesis* that a persistent+discrete MHAC design improves action.

**Falsifier:** S31 already warns that adding sonification can impede a different critical alert. A symbolic oracle forbidding false all-clear cannot resolve that human performance tradeoff. Alternative simpler baseline remains live: discrete alert + visual status. No participant recruitment, restricted source reproduction or study registration took place. AI-assisted recheck; human source verification is not claimed.
