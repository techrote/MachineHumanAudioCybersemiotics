## Decision table

| Later evidence | Implication for MHAC | What would falsify or narrow it |
|---|---|---|
| Authorized and adequately controlled study shows better calibration and timely correct decisions for explicit uncertainty communication | Propose a conditional R013 design hypothesis with accuracy and action endpoints, versioned via actual R012/R014 authorities | Advantage disappears under matched information, time, training, accessible comparisons or different base rates |
| Higher speech trust but unchanged/inferior action | Reject trust/voice-naturalness as a proxy for correctness and prioritize criterion/source/provenance clarity | A robust directional improvement in objective outcomes under correctly matched controls |
| Verification helps only in nonurgent or high-validity conditions | Require explicit checkability/deadline tradeoff and safe fallback rather than universal 'always verify' | Urgent-condition checking is still beneficial after measured delays and opportunity costs |
| No meaningful modality difference with adequate precision | Retain simpler, accessible uncertainty channel; report null without falsely asserting equivalence | Evidence too imprecise or content not matched; repeat rather than conclude |
| Contradictory patterns across prevalence, workload, language, channel or hearing variation | Preserve strata and uncertainties, no pooled 'speech is good/bad' story | Robust effect unchanged across those moderators |
| Simple loss model explains decisions as well as a cybersemiotic interpretation | Record explanatory parsimony in R008/R011 remove-Brier test; retain philosophy only where it adds testable predictions | Account makes new prospective out-of-sample semantic/repair predictions supported by evidence |
| Source validity inaccessible or unknown, or no defensible source identity | **Block** quantitative inference and source acceptance | Actual provenance/eligibility and full critical reading resolved under #5/#6 |

## Unresolved questions and stop conditions

Whether sound-specific effects survive equivalent information and exposure, how correlated independent checks change the model, how speech probability wording interacts with numeracy, whether warning events are genuinely distinct at publication/study/sample level, and whether the non-speech option is teachable and accessible remain unresolved.
