# Prospective uncertainty communication and action protocol — NOT EXECUTED

This is an **R014 handoff proposal only**. No participants, exposure, deception, preregistration, trial data or audio assets were generated. R020's genuine permission, risk, accessibility and ethics gates remain active.

## Questions and prespecified rival hypotheses

- H1: With identical numerical posterior information, speech, text and non-speech auditory mappings will differ in calibration error, correct first consequential action or deadline failures under workload. Null is no meaningful difference within a prespecified precision band, not an automatic equivalence inference.
- H2: Optional independent evidence checking reduces false/incorrect decisions when diagnostically strong and feasible, but increases deadline misses under high temporal cost. A zero or opposite interaction must be retained.
- H3: Alarm false-positive history changes response criterion even when nominal uncertainty wording is held constant. Test alongside a simple signal-detection/expected-loss model.
- H4: Perceived trust, self-rated confidence, probability comprehension, source identification, calibration and correct action need not co-vary. Finding no dissociation would narrow the proposed account.
- Competing prediction: when posterior, task load, sensory access, training and expected losses are controlled, simple decision theory may explain responses without an extra semiotic mechanism.

## Factorial proposal

Use fictional sandbox scenarios with a verifiable source-truth label and no real-world instructions. Factor modality (speech, text with accessible visual alternative, learnable non-speech audio), evidence inspectability (none, optional check, simulated mandatory check), history (low vs high false-alarm prevalence), workload (low vs dual-task) and deadline (generous vs short) with counterbalancing, washout and a justified condition count. The design must not claim every cell feasible; an R014 methodological review should reduce cells and establish power/precision using justified pilot data or simulation.

Control intended semantic content, explicit probability numerals, alarm meaning, truth labels, event timing, opportunity to inspect information, presentation duration and source provenance. Equate or model display/access time, speech rate, training dose, sound level and masking, prosody, task costs and information entropy. Do not force content equivalence by removing accessibility aids; disclose unequal content/access where unavoidable. Include comprehension and manipulation checks for actual likelihood interpretation.

## Measures and analysis specification

- Primary: correct first consequential action by deadline, false act, miss, and failure-to-respond censored at the actual deadline; distinguish from alarm detection.
- Secondary: reported probability (proper score such as Brier score, prespecified direction and handling of missing estimates), absolute calibration/binned reliability with uncertainty intervals, event/source identification, trust ratings, subjective certainty, verification use, verification correctness and time/latency distribution.
- Separately: time-split error (initial vs corrected action), workload, fatigue, hearing-device variation, listening effort, language familiarity and numeracy.
- Analysis: prespecify condition contrasts and hierarchical participant/item dependence, order/history effects, missingness and censoring; compare predictive calibration of a cost-sensitive baseline with independently formulated pragmatic hypotheses. No p-value fishing, significance-as-equivalence or post-hoc index that fuses trust and safety.
- Design decisions: set meaningful loss asymmetries, plausible event base rates and acceptable accuracy/latency margins with documented safety rationale. Do not derive these from synthetic toy values; they are deliberately **not finalized** here.
