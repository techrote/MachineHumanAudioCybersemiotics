## Claim class and verification

Source-attributed empirical reports remain **source claims** with unassessed bias. The Bayesian cost rule is a **methodological tool** and not evidence of human rationality. The notion that natural voice increases inappropriate action is a **plausible cross-task analogy**. The idea that independent verification can both help and delay is a **hypothesis** demonstrated as a mathematical possibility in synthetic scenarios, not a measured listener outcome. Sanderson's urgency/identification dissociation and Manzey's cry-wolf result are **counterevidence** to simple interpretations; neither disproves intentional audio messaging.
