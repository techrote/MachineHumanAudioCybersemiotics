# R014-facing study sketch — proposal, not executed

**Research question.** Does a conditional notification policy reduce interruption cost without degrading the probability of *correct, authorized action before a meaningful deadline*, compared with immediate delivery? Do not optimize status freshness and assume it predicts action.

**Prospective design only:** Before any human contact, require R012/R013 accepted state/action contract, R014 protocol review, R020 authorization/ethics and accessible materials. Predefine audibly and visually matched information, status/event classes and deadlines; independently ground source truth, actor authority and resolved/revoked condition. Compare immediate, modest bounded deferral, workload-boundary with deadline override, and protected coalescing; include a no-adaptation simple comparator and control notification count, actual delivered energy/content, visual redundancy, task demands, device, training and language comprehension. The `latest_wins_naive` policy is an *engineering negative control*, not an ethical hazard condition to expose people to.

**Separate outcomes:** delivered/on-time versus actually detected; semantic identification; correct first action, final action, deadline misses and no-action on revoked events; primary-task error; subjective disruption and accessibility; event recall after interruption. Distinguish unintelligible/undetected signal, incorrect interpretation, authorization errors and task failures. Specify conditional effect sizes/decision thresholds and uncertainty prior to collecting data, including missingness and timeout rules; do not copy the toy's arbitrary ticks as human thresholds.

**Counterpositions and confounders:** task-boundary inference may be erroneous; alerts can increase workload, sound exposure or masking; batching may benefit subjective well-being without timely action; line-of-sight visual alternatives may dominate; no meaningful deferred-delivery use case may exist. Evaluate hearing variation, neurodivergence, language, cultural expectations, reasonable accommodations, low-volume/fallback channels and burden. No recruitment, real alert generation, real participant data, ethics claims or statistical inference is authorized by this document.
