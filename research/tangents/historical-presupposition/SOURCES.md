## Evidence and decision classification

- **Author-reported observations** (S245, S246, S250, S251) apply only within their own reading/eye-tracking/corpus tasks; none is an MHAC empirical finding.
- **Methods** (S247, S248) provide conceptual instruments for provenance and time, not correctness of sensors or comprehension.
- **Counterpositions** (S246, S250, S251, S252) favour contextual and accessibility-sensitive hypotheses over a blanket lexeme blacklist.
- **Speculative mechanism:** a true current-state utterance can carry a historical implication unsupported by a hypothetical machine's available telemetry. The synthetic cases test only logical consistency under declared labels.
