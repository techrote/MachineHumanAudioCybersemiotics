# Six-source exploratory provenance, contradictions and access audit

**Access/reverification date: 2026-11-04 UTC.** Bibliographic identity and abstracts were checked against the linked *primary publisher, author, NLM or institutional pages*. `abstract_only` means metadata and abstract were read, **not** the complete article, detailed tables, formal proof or empirical data. The frozen Track E cutoff (2026-10-04) is unchanged; R005/R006 still govern inclusion, reading sufficiency, study/sample identity and dependence. No paper is being promoted to accepted Track E evidence by this package.

| S153 · parallel empirical | Warren-Noell, H. L., Kaber, D. B. & Sheik-Nainar, M. A. (2006), *Human Performance with Vocal Cueing of Automation State Changes in an Adaptive System*, *Proceedings HFES Annual Meeting* 50(3), 415–419. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/154193120605000343); [publisher abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/154193120605000343). | S154 · parallel nonaudio | Sklar, A. E. & Sarter, N. B. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872099779656716); [NLM PMID 10774125](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10774125/). Abstract: tactile feedback increased mode-transition detection/response under simulated cockpit tasks. | `abstract_only` NLM/publisher. Not direct machine-audio evidence. Source reports supervisory-mode demands and error paths from pilot–automation research. | `abstract_only`, restricted publisher full text. Human-factors mechanism, **not** proof of any audio effect. |
| S156 · orthogonal formal method | Bredereke, J. & Lankenau, A. (2002), *A Rigorous View of Mode Confusion*, SafeComp 2002, LNCS 2434, 19–31. [BrLa05](https://homepages.on.hs-bremen.de/~jbredereke/de/forschung/veroeffentlichungen/BrLa05.html) is a related 2005 journal extension; do not count as independent validation. Formal-method analogy, not behavioral efficacy. |
| S157 · perpendicular counterposition | Björklund, C. M., Alfredson, J. & Dekker, S. W. A. **Evidence classification:** S153/S154/S157/S158 report original authors' task-specific empirical observations within abstract-only access; S155 supplies human-factors framing; S156 a formal methodological tool. MHAC transfer is **plausible analogy or untested design hypothesis**. Negative and null interpretations remain possible. Bibliographic records may refer to overlapping experiments and must be adjudicated by R005/R006. No primary-study effect estimate or participant-level sample has been invented.
