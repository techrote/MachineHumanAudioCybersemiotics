# Four predeclared policies and controls

| Policy | Declared representation (illustrative) | Instrumental failure criterion | Unmeasured liabilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| A \`fused\` | Single sum of 0–2 certainty, hazard and urgency; all missing states become one unknown code. | Multiple state tuples share code; no individually addressable field, including three-field compensating changes. | Meaning conflation deliberately built in; cannot infer attention/response. |
| B \`naive\` | Numeric pitch in Hz for certainty (300/500/700), relative level index 0/1/2 for hazard and timbre index 0/1/2 for urgency. | Equal codes from different truth states, non-monotonic scaling or unaffected field changing. | Pitch–level integration and contextual conceptual polarity; **relative level is NOT calibrated dB SPL**. |
| C \`engineered\` | Illustrative brightness, roughness and pulse-rate indices 1/2/3 for certainty, hazard and urgency respectively. | Same structural tests as B. Distinct parameter names alone do not establish perceptual orthogonality. | Psychoacoustic interactions, masking, temporal workload and gain/tolerance constraints; not actual Ziemer synthesis. |
| D \`serial\` | Explicit separately named fields and fixed action/addressee tokens; possible text/speech/accessible-channel equivalent. | Missing/ambiguous field label or duplicate symbolic code. | Longer duration, intelligibility, language, hearing, urgency deadline, rendering and comprehension untested. |

All numeric ranges are **unvalidated engineering placeholders**, not human intensity ratings or approved audio settings. Deliberately defective mapping variants are injected in tests but never treated as fifth policy: reversed certainty scale and saturated hazard scale must fail validation before result computation.

Unknown handling is not synonymous with low certainty; no fixed globally intuitive acoustic vocabulary is proposed. Correlated and anti-correlated field values, individual-field changes, a three-way collision, and serial alternatives are represented in the full factorial. Check pairwise/single-variable invariance *and* entire 3D combinations: two-dimensional success in a study is not evidence of joint three-field MHAC interpretation.

This is a mapping-*specification* comparison, not a test of signal amplitude at the ear, JND, listener response, fatigue, language comprehension or correct action. No engineered-policy superiority may be claimed from equal unique-code counts.
