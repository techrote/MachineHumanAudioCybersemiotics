# Conditional R013/R014 protocol sketch — **NOT EXECUTED**

**Question:** For independently truth-labelled certainty, hazard and urgency fields, do individuals correctly decode **each named semantic field** and choose the authorized consequential action under finite deadlines? Compare simultaneous naïve/engineered candidate mappings, serial speech/text, and no multifeature code. Physical injectivity alone is not an endpoint.

**Prerequisites:** R005/R006 source eligibility and identity appraisal as applicable, accepted R012 truth contract and R013 candidate hypotheses; R014 protocol approval and real R020 owner/ethics/participant authorization before **any** listening, recruitment or sound exposure. The frozen 2026-10-04 eligibility cutoff and amendment process remain intact; this is not a preregistration. This document is a proposed work item, not a participant plan authorized to execute.

**Design if later authorized:** randomize/counterbalance policy ordering, train on the same semantic payload with recorded cue exposure and retention interval. Present 1-field, pairwise and held-out 3-field cases; contrast positively correlated and anticorrelated variables, unexpected unknowns, incongruent mapping, dual-task attention and competing audio. Control voice/language, cue duration, intensity safety and speaker/headphone path. Predeclare exclusion/missingness handling and accessibility alternatives without treating hearing, age or culture as interchangeable categories.

**Distinct outcomes:**
1. Physical feature preservation and code collision (engineering test).
2. Identification of an acoustic dimension (perception).
3. Attribution to the *correct independent truth field* (semantic decoding).
4. Wrong first consequential action, wrong recipient/unauthorized action, and eventual correct action (behavior).
5. Deadline omission, task resumption and completion time (timeliness).
6. Training effort, delayed retention, perceived workload and acoustic tolerance (cost/acceptability).

**Negative controls and falsifiers:** decode single fields but misassign simultaneous fields; handle familiar pairs but fail held-out triples; produce better detection times but more wrong-first actions; show gain in quiet yet failure under noise, divided attention or channel processing; speech or serial representation dominates when no simultaneous information is needed. Define per-participant independence before any pooling; no convenience assumption about samples or independent trials.

**Analysis sketch:** hierarchical or clustered analysis with participant-level uncertainty, prespecified per-field and per-policy contrasts, conditional error matrices, and explicit non-pooling if tasks are not comparable. For strong effects require full source and human-protocol verification; report null/contrary effects and accessibility exclusions. No invented power calculation or target sample size without design/variance justification.

**Decision rule:** if independently controlled parameters remain perceptually integral or are semantically confused, reduce simultaneous dimensions or use serialized accessible alternatives. If no use case requires joint codes, do not build an acoustic vocabulary. Successful physics or synthetic fixtures cannot license any deployment claim.
