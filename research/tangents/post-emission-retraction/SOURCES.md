## Interpretative boundaries and opposing views

- **Established in source-specific settings:** human speakers mark repairs; experimental corrections can alter or fail to eliminate later inferences; formal workflow vocabularies distinguish cancellation and completion. These cannot establish a quantified MHAC warning failure.
- **Plausible analogy:** an issued communication, receipt acknowledgement, correction offer, actor action and physical recovery should be modelled as different statements of evidence.
- **Falsifiable hypothesis:** a careless sender policy could treat issuance of an apology or cancellation as proof that the recipient stopped or that damage was reversed. The fictional comparator deliberately makes this wrong inference to test rejection.
- **Counterposition:** a minimal identity-bound retraction without explicit lengthy uncertainty text might achieve identical *structural truthfulness* while imposing fewer text tokens. A simpler outbound-only status interface might not need a full corrective lifecycle.
- **No direct transfer:** neither speech repair nor misinformation narrative designs show correct physical-action timing, reversibility, receipt or acoustic intelligibility under machine alarm conditions.
