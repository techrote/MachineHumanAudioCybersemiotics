| Inconclusive / unmeasurable (`null` ILD after mono, undefined zero-signal spectral ratio, unmodeled receiver) | The feature is not identifiable using that observation channel. | Treat as **unknown**, not zero error or success. Do not infer semantics. |

## Alternatives against premature codebook complexity

(A) Predeclare redundant orthogonal acoustic cues but test for correlations after the channel; (B) speak the source, state and action explicitly rather than infer field binding from timbre/spatiality; (C) provide text/haptic or other accessible non-audio fallback without presuming the audio has been heard; (D) reduce the number of distinct categories to what survives in a realistic receiving environment. **None is shown superior by this synthetic model.** Any choice needs independent evidence and applicable accessibility requirements.

## Follow-on discrimination (proposed only)

When R005/R006 evidence has been screened, R008 can compare a mundane physical-channel explanation to richer semiotic theories, and R009 can retain separate original human outcome measures. Under R012/R013 accepted semantics, R014 could preregister a safe and authorized comparison of *correct cue detection*, *first source/event identification*, *urgency interpretation*, *first wrong action*, *deadline omission*, *eventual correct action* and *effort*, stratified by hearing/device/language/training and room position. Use independent ground-truth source/action records; do not equate a recognizable sound with authentic authority. Actual participant testing, calibration and recruitment remain exclusively under #20 after explicit human authorization.

## Scope closure and human gates

This package is a synthetic-only proof of **instrumental failure possibilities**. It neither amends the frozen protocol nor authorizes a production voice engine, clinical hearing-aid recommendations, human listening, participant work or external publication. R005/R006 source-identity and formal appraisal remain blocked by their own evidence/access conditions; T007 can be reviewed on its independent engineering/research acceptance surface. CI verifies deterministic code and record integrity, not scientific effectiveness or listener safety.
