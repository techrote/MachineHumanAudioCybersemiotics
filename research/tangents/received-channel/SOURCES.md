## Identity and decision status

- Publication-to-study-to-sample relationships **unadjudicated**, especially Regev 2023/2024/2025 and May 2018 vs Kowalewski 2020. Do **not** count citations as independent effect estimates. R005/R006 own eventual formal screening and full-text checks.
- No study reports these ten fictional signals or their numeric proxy outputs. Every numeric result in this directory is produced by the local deterministic Python model, not extracted human data.
- **Methodological use**: screen a proposed acoustic mapping for *instrumental* feature collapses or rank sensitivity before asking humans to judge meaning. Reject a mapping when the declared synthetic diagnostic fails; passing does not establish comprehension or safe action.
- Alternatives: redundant cue dimensions, explicit spoken source/action fields, non-audio fallback or a smaller codebook may dominate elaborate sonification. None has measured comparative advantage here.
- No restricted paper, copyrighted figure, participant data, or device algorithm is included. Further reading must record exact methods/results/table locators and independently adjudicate source identity before use as accepted evidence.
