# MHAC-T003 decision memo — candidate protocol, not adoption

**3 November 2026 UTC.** The state-machine and **39 fictional traces** were built as an adversarial rehearsal, not as an empirical source, participant record or formal verification of a deployed system. A successful deterministic test proves only the tested invariant under the invented event model.

## Observed model capability

The rehearsal preserves distinct delivery report, reported interpretation, acknowledgement, action report, timeliness, and resolution. It rejects mismatched source/event/version, old answers, duplicate request IDs, unproven unknown-field promotion and structurally invalid transitions **without mutating state**. It explicitly records cannot_answer, no fallback, elapsed deadlines, late completion, pending-request expiry and invalidation on version changes. Synthetic constants are arbitrary and must not be taken into #12–#14 as calibrated limits.

## Conditional decision table

| Inconclusive reading/eligibility or insufficient human evaluation | Remain exploratory; no normative timing, evidence promotion, R012 schema change, or release claim. |

- **#8/#11:** compare grounded interaction with simpler procedural/workload models and the remove-Brier test. Reuse the existing R004 Clark–Brennan record, not a duplicate.
- **#12:** evaluate optional source/event/version/request correlation and explicit unknown/acknowledgement distinctions as **proposed conventions**, with provenance and objections; no silent modification to accepted schema.
- **#13:** fair A/B/C/D information-equivalent design candidates with button/text access, not always-on ASR or anthropomorphic dialogue.
- **#14:** preregistration-ready correct-action-by-deadline study design including omissions, interferences, attribution and falsifiers; separate computational proof from human results.
- **#30:** maintain observation-stage taxonomy; a missed action alone cannot establish masking, inattention or comprehension.
- **#31:** cannot_answer is not a probability or calibrated trust signal; avoid conflating fluency, certainty and correctness.
- **#20:** human gate remains unmet; no real listening, participant recruitment, device control, external review or registration.

## Counterevidence and unresolved limitations

The IBM pages were read at abstract level, and the R004 chapter **metadata**, not full theoretical passages, were inspected. No full critical extraction, independent study-identity adjudication or evidence synthesis occurred. Related aviation research warns against treating modality redundancy as an unconditional benefit; no available paper here tests deadline-governed recipient repair itself. The finite, author-selected synthetic cases are not exhaustive state-space verification. Concurrent alarms, distributed sensor correctness, real-time latency, hearing/motor/language variation and safety consequences remain untested.
