# Provisional disposition — synthetic logic, no adoption

**Current stage:** limited GO for issue #62's *logical authority boundary* and a **NO-GO for claims about actual MHAC listener performance**. Research artifacts are designed to expose three separate mistakes: an unauthorized verbal class, a real-world false decision despite nominal rule compliance, and an action instruction without separate authority.

### Interpretation and limitations

A nominal specification cannot stand in for an authenticated selected decision rule. A selected rule by itself cannot authorize a renderer to invent a classification. A valid upstream classification still does **not** imply a safe real-world state or “no action needed.” The simple value-only alternative can avoid unsupported classes and action offers; its possible omission of justified classifications is a trade-off, not proof of inferiority.

In `T017-002` and `006`, warranted decisions are deliberately wrong relative to fictional world truth; any reporting that calls D universally *correct* would conceal those false decisions. In `029`/`030`, the same number can have different explicitly **chosen** rule outcomes. The values and risk labels are fabricated, so **no risk model** or universal 49/51 guard band follows from the contrast.

**Decision consequences:** 
- If the falsifier exposes unauthorized classes not covered elsewhere: propose an R008 conventional *decision-authority* comparator and only a future R012 typed provenance **hypothesis**.
- If authorized classes are also wrong about the world: reject any implication that rule provenance proves real-world safety; keep false-accept and false-reject costs visible.
- If value-only is simpler or the accepted programme never admits numeric threshold classes: recommend no new audio/semantic subsystem.
- If evidence is ambiguous or source authority cannot be verified: retain uncertainty rather than guessing distributions or risk thresholds.

No change to accepted empirical records, protocol, programme, frozen cutoff, or action policy. Human comprehension or safety benefit can only be assessed after independently sourced design, authorization and R020 human gates.
