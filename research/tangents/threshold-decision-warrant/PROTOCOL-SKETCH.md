# Prospective R014/R020 hypothesis sketch — **not executed**

**Precondition:** accepted synthesis and R012/R013 contracts explicitly require spoken, measurement-derived conformity classification; R005/R006 establishes relevant evidence, and an owner authorizes the R020 human pilot and its ethics/accessibility plan. Otherwise **STOP**. The present experiment has no human participants, no waveform recordings and no real-device safety outcome.

Hypothesis: holding source, event, information and urgency fixed, an **authorized and qualified** numeric-class message might improve *correct interpretation of the rule-specific decision* relative to a value-only message, without increasing false “safe” inference. The alternative is that a numeric report plus inspectable text performs equally or better, especially in noisy, multilingual, unfamiliar-unit or divided-attention conditions. None of these effects is currently observed.

Prospective randomized/counterbalanced within-person design: construct independently audited real-world-plausible *safe simulated* scenarios; compare A, D, and possibly a conventional speech reference. Factor state (clear, near-boundary, stale, unknown uncertainty), authorization validity and acoustic noise; match numeric fidelity, voice, timing and training. Primary outcomes: distinction between numeric measurement, asserted decision class and claimed **real-world** safety, plus unauthorized-action errors and incorrect rejection. Report failed/omitted responses and deadline misses, not just success latency. Secondary: recognition of number/unit, workload, correction requests, learned interpretation, subjective acceptability. Include hearing variation, language, numeracy, alternative text, masking and training exposure. Decide the smallest meaningful effect and precision *before* sample-size calculation; no guessed n or effect size.

**Falsifiers:** no benefit over value-only; increased false safety in qualified-class condition; confusion of “accepted under rule” with physical safety; longer spoken claims delaying action; inaccessible modality. Stop or narrow rather than post-hoc claim success. Preregistration, participant recruitment, ethics approvals and external submission remain unauthorized. R008 conventional decision theory must be evaluated before assigning any incremental explanatory value to cybersemiotics.
