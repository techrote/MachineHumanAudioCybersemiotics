**Source integrity and independence:** JCGM is a guide, Phillips–Krystek a methodological paper; do not count them as participant experiments. Hull, Lambert, Mandel and Shawahna study different tasks and populations; do not pool, generalize quantitative effects to MHAC or count indexed records as primary full-text readings. The **working hypothesis**, not a research fact, is that a spoken derived classification needs separately traceable decision authority. Conditional implementation is an R008 conventional decision-theory challenge to claims of unique semiotic leverage. No Brier endorsement is claimed.
