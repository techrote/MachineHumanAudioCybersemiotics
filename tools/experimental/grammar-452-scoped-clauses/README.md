## Language contract

Five ordered four-valued semantic fields are exported as names and words in lab.cjs: source (pump/door/fan/relay), event (fault/open/stopped/overload), polarity (affirmed/negated/unknown/conflicting), confidence (unassessed/low/medium/high), scope (report/question/quotation/request-verification). All 1,024 combinations are legal *synthetic syntax*, not physically plausible assertions. Confidence concerns the embedded content and is a nominal label, not a calibrated probability. The scope applies to the entire four-field proposition: a quotation or question is not an assertion by the sender. No nested scopes, quoted speaker identity, event version, multiple-source binding, response, action execution, timing validity or real-device mapping; no claim of operational safety. An unknown polarity with high confidence is allowed as a convention, not a factual diagnosis.

Example: [0,0,1,3,2] represents a quotation of high-confidence negated pump fault; changing only the final semantic digit to 0 makes it a report, 1 a question. These are different speech acts despite identical embedded content. The parity slots are not semantic tokens. Fixed position binds roles; receiver assumes the 700 ms frame boundary and a shared grammar version. No synchronization or channel authentication.

## Acoustic codec and exact recovery

Mono 8 kHz signed PCM16, seven 100 ms slots (80 ms tapered sinusoid, 20 ms guard). Digits 0..6 map to 400..1000 Hz in 100 Hz steps. Peak 4096/32768, approximately -18 dBFS; no loudness calibration or hearing-safety guarantee. Playback is optional, owner-initiated at low volume. Two check slots over the prime field GF(7) use P=-sum(x_i), Q=-sum(i*x_i), i=1..5. Decoder enumerates at most 49 assignments for up to two known erasures and accepts only a unique legal codeword with both zero syndromes; it refuses more than two erasures even if the remaining data would suffice. Any two columns of H = [(1,1)..(1,5),(1,0),(0,1)] are linearly independent mod 7. Therefore distance >=3 and every pair of known erasures is uniquely recoverable; any single substitution with no erasure is rejected. The retained zero-vs-[1,0,0,0,0] witness gives distance exactly 3 and proves a three-erasure collision. Two substitutions plus an erasure can also yield a valid wrong clause; the receipt retains an explicit example. No automatic single-error correction: refusal is the chosen policy. No general guarantee beyond these conditions.

Waveform receiver uses phase-aligned normalized dot products with the seven rendered tone templates, an energy floor of 5% of reference, a best correlation >=0.8 and a best/runner-up gap >=0.2; otherwise a slot is marked erased. Exact sample alignment, known polarity/phase and clean synthetic inputs are assumed. Unknown phase, gain below floor, frequency drift, noise, reverb, overlap, clipping and frame misalignment not qualified. Silence is an erasure, not a semantic digit.

## Reproduce / compare

Node.js 18+ (standard library only): `node lab.cjs output`. Writes qualification.json and five original 700 ms WAVs (quotation/report/question, two erased fields, unprotected quotation). Compare the generated receipt with the committed one; floating-point sinusoid rounding may vary across engines at exact ties. The callable API exports encode, decode, baseline, semantic, render, receive, wav, qualify, specimens and vocabulary. Example: `node -e "const g=require('./lab.cjs'); console.log(g.semantic(g.decode(g.receive(g.render(g.encode([0,0,1,3,2])))).message))"`.

Matched 700 ms unprotected serial baseline: same five semantic tones, final two slots silent. Same nominal frequency band and duration, but protected mode uses two extra tones and approximately 40% more energy (equal nominal amplitude, seven vs five active slots); not an equal-energy comparison or a human performance experiment. Unpadded serial would take 500 ms. Uniform erasure-mask census includes padded slots: one known erasure 7168/7168 vs baseline 2048/7168; two 21504/21504 vs 1024/21504. No statistical inference: exhaustive finite cases. For clean held-out composition, train the *symbol dictionary* with all 256 messages whose field sum is 0 mod 4; all individual tokens appear, remaining 768 combinations are withheld. Compositional receiver is given grammar and all primitive mappings by construction; a flat lookup-only oracle is defined to know only the 256 trained strings and abstains on the remaining 768. This is a representational capacity comparison, not learned generalization, and no flat acoustic renderer or learning model is implemented. No claim of priority for parity codes or compositional earcons.
