## INST-010E — bounded voice replacement (26 November 2026)

This 0.1.1 candidate changes overflow admission, not AC01, the five stable parameter IDs, class IDs, or the receipt law. The low-level Engine retains rejection by default; Live opts into replacement. Use a free slot, then the oldest releasing slot, then oldest held slot. A held victim is cancelled and cannot later commit history. A releasing victim has already committed or cancelled; replacement does not alter its past result. Phase, weights and gain of the displaced voice are retained for a forced fade of at most approximately 5 ms. A new voice attacks at the same slope, keeping their envelope sum at most one (roundoff aside). Eight slots, at most eight extra fading voices, no allocation, lock or I/O in processing. A slot already crossfading is ineligible; if every slot is crossfading, reject rather than truncate a tail or allocate. The nine-token live map permits a spare internal token during replacement, invalidates stolen held IDs, and ignores their later note-off. Hosts that reuse the same ID or channel/pitch fallback key before an old note-off cannot disambiguate it; overlapping identical held keys remain rejected. Reset removes both active and fading voices.
