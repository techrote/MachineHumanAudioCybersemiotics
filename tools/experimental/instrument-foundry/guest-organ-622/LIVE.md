## Playing and controls
The main mono/stereo monitor and two optional mono stems (Base and Counter) use the original synthesis. Mute the monitor when mixing stems independently. Eight bounded note slots including release tails; no arbitrary stealing. Same-ID note-on during release starts a fresh local phrase in a free slot while the old tail finishes; duplicate held identifiers and all new notes when all slots are occupied are rejected. Note-off affects the held instance, not a prior releasing tail. No claim of unlimited retrigger or click-free audio. All controls are sampled at onset; changing adoption during a note does not change its rule. Citation remains acoustically neutral.

IDs 1200–1203: motif intervals -12..12 semitones, normalized (interval+12)/24. 1204: speed 1..12 steps/s, normalized (speed-1)/11. 1205: counter gain 0..1. 1206: citation flag. 1207: adoption flag. Default motif 0,7,3,10; speed 4; gain .65; flags off. Try motif 0,5,-2,9, rate 7, gain .35, adoption on, or 0,0,7,3 at 2 steps/s and gain .9. GO01 patch state: 4 magic bytes plus eight little-endian binary32 normalized values; discrete values quantized on read; active voices not serialized. Restore resets voices. No concurrent state access qualification; serialized lifecycle required in this candidate.
