# INST-011N — offline spectral-depth residual census

Refs #738; creative synthesis originates in #265 at 56c107f8f4147316a588484c47be28bc8f840fbb. This is an engineering measurement instrument, not a human listening experiment. Qualified binary input: 340c1ae14fc0e52495f2eaa03385db4013ba5bf0 (Windows run 45988761155, artifact 15192213198).

Build the existing native CMake project and run `orbit_sideband_probe > sidebands.csv` (use the Release executable on multi-config generators). CTest runs the same probe. No SDK or third-party FFT dependency is required for this offline executable. The plugin audio path is unchanged. The exact SDK lock still applies to plugin builds.

The probe renders paired single-note depth-zero/depth-full performances with long-orbit blend, velocity 0.8, MIDI notes 69/93/105/117/125, releases 10/80/800 ms, all three release modes, two note-off offsets (100 ms plus 17 or 53 samples at 48 kHz, scaled by four at 192 kHz), and rates 48/192 kHz. It checks exact pre-release identity, finite bounded output, final silence, linear-mode invariance and exact fallback when the octave is excluded. A radix-two FFT with zero padding measures the stereo difference across the complete release, not the sustained note. A two-tone energy oracle and silence oracle verify bin classification and Parseval normalization; each transform also checks Parseval. The RMS denominator includes eight trailing silent samples.

The reported `above_24k_fraction` is the fraction of paired residual energy above 24 kHz at the measurement rate, not the fraction of original output energy or a direct measurement of aliasing at 48 kHz. At 48 kHz the fraction is necessarily zero by definition. The `cutoff_matched` column identifies equal harmonic admission at both rates. MIDI 69 and 93 have all 34 long-orbit harmonics admitted at both rates; 105, 117 and 125 do not. Different cutoffs can create genuine extra carrier energy in the high-rate reference; exclude those cases from the matched-cutoff proxy. Phase-route selection and discrete envelope sampling are rate-dependent; the high-rate rendering is not a proven time-aligned bandlimited oracle. No general alias or inaudibility guarantee is established.
