# Intervention-conditioned sustain (INST-011S)

Refs #738, #264 and draft #265 at 56c107f8f4147316a588484c47be28bc8f840fbb, Exact intervention compiler and Synthetic census. Creative synthesis interpretation, not an intervention-minimal solver, physical model, listening result or human evidence.

The existing 68 phase-conditioned escape energy routes are used as time-expanded modulation shapes. Each note may opt into its own route, depth [0,1] and period [200,8000] ms before its first rendered sample. The route is read forward and backward, beginning at normalized energy 1, reaching zero at the midpoint, and returning continuously to 1. It is not a reversible integer-state trajectory. With interpolated energy E, intervention intensity I = depth*(1-E). Output gain is scaled by 1-0.65*I and oscillator B blend becomes blend+(1-blend)*I. Independent note phases and periods produce evolving chords without additional oscillators. Release retains the sustain phase and uses the existing independently selected release policy. Depth zero preserves the previous path; no new state or parameter IDs are introduced. This is currently a native opt-in API, not a DAW control or new VST3 release.

SustainPath validates all active knots before copying them. Fixed storage, bounded configure, constant-work tick, no allocation, locks or I/O in rendering. Engine::noteSustain accepts an existing newly admitted voice only, rejects repeats and invalid requests without mutation. Route indices are selected by the caller; phase-conditioned release remains independent. The ping-pong shape is continuous but slope changes and sidebands are possible; no alias-free claim.
