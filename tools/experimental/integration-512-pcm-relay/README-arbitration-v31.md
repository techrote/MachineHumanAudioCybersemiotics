## Contract

Unmodified v29 mandatory speech-act PCM and v30 durable receiver with isolated repeat-confirmation credits. One nonpreemptive shared channel, known observation boundaries of 55,936 samples (3.496 s; nominal frame 3.28 s), horizon 1,678,080 samples (104.88 s). First four slots identical across policies: two old effects committed, B's ACK lost; then trusted local epoch grant and exact closure grants. New offers have distinct sample-time arrivals (not aligned to frame boundaries). Arbitration occurs at frame completion or next available event. Two DATA and two CLOSE attempts per transaction, timeout after two slot durations following transmission. At attempt exhaustion wait for any matching receipt until deadline/horizon; no new transmission. Eight queued jobs max; at most six offers. Responses separately queued after strict decoded receiver decisions; ACK/CONFIRM associations require decoded identity, AST and role. No oracle decides receipt. Scheduler is centralized, with out-of-band job priority/deadline and access to sender state, and evaluator fault controls/labels; no distributed MAC, arbitrary stream acquisition, actual overlap, clarification scheduling or source authentication. A simulated effect is a durable counter, not a physical action. Queue pruning includes stale jobs and deadline expiry, not exclusively deadline misses.

Policies: FIFO, responses first then priority, emission debt, receipt debt, and response-first with ordering guard. Both debt policies accrue one slot per occupied slot with outstanding source demand; rank 4*(priority+2 for responses)+debt/slot. They differ only in debt reset (any source emission vs matching sender transition); idle time not counted, debt can clear despite other pending obligations from same source. Ordering guard blocks newer DATA for same source/epoch until older DATA has a matching ACK, expires or exhausts attempts and timeout. Guard does not modify receiver or framing, and is not a delivery guarantee. No imported upstream code. Conceptual comparator from #457 service-debt.js/README-service-debt.md at deff7227805ef5c2f9970e9d12d66a7ee05f062c, which counts emitted packet completion, not PCM receipt; not a reproduction of its factorial.

## Results and retained failures

Five scenarios x five policies x two deterministic phase seeds = 50 sessions. Per policy 56 offers, 20 old settlement opportunities, 10 D offers. FIFO 50 applications / 48 ACK confirmations / 20 settlements; response-first, emission debt and receipt debt each 48/48/20; ordered response 52/52/20. Urgent D confirmed 8/10 FIFO, 10/10 each other policy. Debt variants differ in some timing from response-first but no difference in these aggregate outcome counts. In pressure, response-first sends E before older same-source C, high-water receiver rejects both C attempts as stale. FIFO and ordering guard recover C. In tight, FIFO misses D; ordered response confirms D at 27.968 s and C at 55.936 s. In loss, C first role suffix erased and D first ACK dropped; ordered response recovers all six, C 73.416 s and E 80.408 s. In blocked, both C DATA attempts erased, E deadline 45.448 s: response-first confirms E at 41.952 s; ordered response never applies E. FIFO applies E but fails to confirm it. This is a head-of-line blocking counterexample: retaining ordering makes a later communication depend on an earlier one's resolution/timeout. No universal ranking. All 20 old obligations settle under all policies. Phase seeds replicate outcomes, not independent population samples. No general fairness, adversarial security or human interpretation claim.
