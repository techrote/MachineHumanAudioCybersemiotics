## Method and scope

For each fixed body-window/clock hypothesis, let Z be the observed complex Fourier sum and E observed energy. Under the explicit completion model abs(missing sample) <= M, missing Fourier contributions are contained in an axis-aligned rectangle of half widths M*sum(abs(cos)) and M*sum(abs(sin)) over missing indices. Total energy lies in [E,E+m*M*M]. Use nearest/farthest rectangle distances to bound the original Goertzel score 2*abs(Z_full)^2/(n*E_full), accounting for the 250-count RMS gate. A symbol is stable if its lower score >=0.85 and lower score minus every rival upper score >=0.2; erasure is certified only if every upper score <0.85. Real-arithmetic sufficient bounds, not necessary bounds; floating point padding is an engineering margin, not formal directed-rounding proof. M is a stipulated bound, NOT inferred from observed audio. PCM16 unconditional bound is 32768 counts. No missing sample reads. No changed wire, acquisition, scheduler or receipt rule. The audit applies to successful diagnostic hypotheses only, not all alternatives or detection/cluster/release history. associationCertified is always false. No added persistent PCM ring; per audit O(5*17*n) per missing body window, trace payload extra and caller retained; no embedded runtime claim.

## Results

21 matched two-packet fixtures (three seeds, seven conditions), 207 missing-window instances across 69 valid diagnostic clock hypotheses, not independent packet trials. At M=128/512/1024/4096/32768 counts, stable windows 131/91/90/45/0, all missing windows stable within hypothesis 41/30/30/15/0. Three 4-sample holes: 45/45 windows stable at 4096; three 16-sample holes: 0/45 at 4096, 45/45 at 1024. Three 64-sample holes: no stable windows even at 128, conservative bound inconclusive. Post-frame gap fixtures have no missing body windows but strict receiver still withholds first packet; no association promotion from vacuous body completeness. Reproduced v15 clean/altered/masked witness: two distinct complete-capture decisions, identical masked diagnostic traces; no stable missing windows at any cap. These findings do not establish that lower bounds are true of unobserved audio, or that full receiver decisions are invariant.
