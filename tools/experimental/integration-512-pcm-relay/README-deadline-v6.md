## Experiment

V5 confounded turnaround with absolute timer duration. Four comparators share the 320,000-sample (20 s, 16 kHz) horizon, 32,000-sample nominal transmit opportunity, 11 scenario definitions, three arbitration policies and at most two DATA attempts per record. Zero guard has fixed absolute timers. Fixed guard adds 640 samples (40 ms) to every nominal slot, with either period-scaled or absolute timers. Absolute retry is 96,000 samples after DATA start; return ready and expiry use 32,000 samples times the same scenario delay/TTL factors from the known nominal transmission epoch. Adaptive uses absolute timers and waits in 16-sample steps while the actual Stream has an onset cluster or pending candidate; at most 640 extra samples per opportunity, never waits on overflow. The nominal grid is known to the shared-channel simulation; variable epochs are recorded, not passed to the PCM decoder. Adaptive is coupled to v4 internal acquisition state, not an independently qualified interface; observation does not expose intended payload or future samples. Existing public checksum and forged-ACK weaknesses persist. Faults and carrier phase follow the original slot-index fixtures, not an identical absolute-time noise field. This is a paired deterministic synthetic corpus, not population estimates or physical real-time qualification.

All modes: 51 unique receipts, three false confirmations, 14 expired returns. All 33 fixed-scaled/absolute paired waveform hashes are identical: the timer extension does not explain the v5 retry improvement in this fixture set. Adaptive uses 3.495 s rather than 11.880 s aggregate scheduled guard (70.58% reduction), but does not create additional complete opportunities in this fixed horizon. Guard count includes the reservation after each nominal opportunity, even the last. In clean ACK-first, confirmations occur at 4.054625/8.113625 s adaptive vs 4.067625/8.149625 s fixed, and 6.028625/8.029625 s zero. No universal latency or throughput dominance. Missed-next-boundary excludes cases without a subsequent opportunity; lateEvidence counts release after nominal end irrespective of guard. No accepted result at terminal EOF. Silence is modeled; no computational wall-clock or embedded turnaround promise.
