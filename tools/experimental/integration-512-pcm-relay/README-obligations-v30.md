## Experimental contract

Use unchanged mandatory v29 DATA/ACK/CLOSE/CONFIRM framing, 16 kHz, 52,480 nominal frame samples (3.28 s), 55,936 samples per observation slot; no fallback or new wire format. Three old transactions A (source 1, generation 0), B (source 2, generation 0), C (source 1, generation 1); D in new epoch 1. Six scenarios, three policies, two phase seeds 71/113, 16 fixed DATA/return slot pairs per session (111.872 s). Sender role and attempt depend on actually decoded matching responses; fixtures schedule offers, grants, injection, loss and reopen; no asynchronous scheduler or independent clocks. Source/role/epoch/generation/AST recovered from PCM, local authorization and context version configured separately. Logical labels and injection provenance evaluator-only. Receiver effect is a persisted counter, never a real action. Explicit exact-record local grant is trusted out-of-band input, not cryptographic authentication or evidence that a sender received an ACK.

Receiver stores up to four records per epoch, eight total, active/draining/tombstone phases, grant bit, exact packet representation, per-record repeat credit and shared pool, all in a single checksummed atomic replacement. One writer; 8 KiB input bound. Closure retires only the matching record. New epoch DATA can be processed while old obligations remain. Next epoch grant blocked while any record drains; when allowed, evicts all prior tombstones. A valid prior snapshot can still be restored. Duplicate DATA ACKs are not rate-limited in this experiment. First CLOSE-CONFIRM is uncharged; only repeat CLOSE responses consume credits. Public-pool permits ungranted closure, local-pool requires grant, local-isolated requires grant and partitions repeat allowance. Shared pool of three and one per each of three old records have identical initial repeat budget for the compared sessions; not matched for other record counts. No general replay defense or fairness theorem.

Across both seeds (12 sessions per policy, 36 old transactions each): public-pool 26 settlements / 4 premature forged closures; local-pool 28 / 2; local-isolated 30 / 2. All policies 48 simulated applications and 12 new-epoch confirmations. B settled in 2/12, 4/12, 6/12 respectively. Nominal transmitted samples 10705920 / 10915840 / 10600960 (lost and injected frames included). No population inference. Before-grant forgery refused only by local policies; matching forgery after grant still retires B before its lost ACK can be recovered. Cross-record replay of A exhausts the pooled allowance; partitioning admits one replay response for A, reserves one for B and one for C, and B recovers its lost confirmation. Same-record replay consumes B's credit and all policies fail. All-confirmation-loss leaves B uncertain under all policies. All receiver records close even when B's sender remains uncertain. Capacity controls fill four records, refuse fifth and conflicting same-key DATA, block further transition while draining, close individually, advance and refuse an old CLOSE after tombstone eviction. This last is an interface counterexample, not an observed sender recovery under a third transition.

Interpretation: authority to close a particular record, authority to spend its repair budget, and evidence of remote settlement are independent. Partitioning protects against inter-record interference under this matched finite workload, not against indistinguishable same-record requests or channel saturation. Local preauthorization narrows the time window for premature closure but cannot authenticate matching messages. Further work: bounded DATA/ACK replay response, authenticated closure or externally justified authority, explicit sender uncertainty during tombstone eviction, fair actual asynchronous slot scheduling, restart/rollback and sustained workloads.
