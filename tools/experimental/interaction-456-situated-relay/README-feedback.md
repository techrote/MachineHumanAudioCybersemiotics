## Contract
Two 250 ms slots per packet, source-level debt ranking from previous lab, one pending per source; old active packet can finish but receiver rejects stale versions. New observations precede due feedback at tick boundaries. At completion the synthetic oracle knows the current version and samples loss via deterministic integer hash of seed/source/version/attempt; forced drop at second tick overrides. Reliable delayed out-of-band feedback has no return-channel cost. Optimistic claims delivery after emission; confirmed requires live receipt feedback; repair additionally queues one retry if current and enough time remains. A receipt is neither human acknowledgement nor action or resolution. Pending feedback beyond horizon retained. Same forward channel budget/renderer, actual occupancy differs. Audio is a motif illustration, not waveform decoding. No human evidence, real-device control or authentication.
