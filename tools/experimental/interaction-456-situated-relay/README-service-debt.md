## Model
Original three policies are called directly from the unchanged attention.js. New nonpreemptive EDF, debt and debt-guard share its arrivals, packet length (two 250 ms ticks), bounded queue, completion deadlines and renderer. Source debt increments each tick with queued unserved demand, survives replacement, clears on a completed packet or absent demand. Debt rank is 4*priority+debt; guard promotes priority-2 packets with fewer than four ticks left before deadline, then applies debt rank. EDF sorts by deadline; ties specified in code. No universal fairness guarantee: a guard can starve other sources and future arrivals cannot be anticipated. These are scheduling rules, not semantic decoding or human attention. Gap metric is largest interval from first offer through completions and horizon, includes idle periods and censored tail; do not interpret as latency. Completed latency in raw trace is conditional on completion. Same nominal gain and pulse length; occupancy and energy vary. PCM peak 2334/32768, playback default 0.15, not calibrated SPL.
