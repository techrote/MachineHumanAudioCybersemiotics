Sampling phase observatory

Refs #442 / #428. Companion to receipt inspector v1. Synthetic measurement only. Use low hardware volume; playback is opt-in; no SPL or listening-safety claim.

Sample phase is a common sub-sample offset before quantisation, not independent onset jitter or clock drift. Uniform deletion index and independent uniform continuous phase on [0,1). Known-phase receiver observes the phase as extra information; hidden-phase receiver marginalises it. Both observe exact ordered indices, not noisy waveform detections.

Method, provenance and qualification

Quantiser: pad + floor(n/d + phase). Every breakpoint is (d - n mod d)/d for noninteger n/d. Exact rational comparison partitions [0,1) into half-open intervals; observations constant on each. Known accuracy is interval-weighted distinct receipts / 4; hidden accuracy is sum of largest joint deletion mass per receipt. Entropies are conditional Shannon entropies in bits. Integration weights and entropies are floating-point; boundaries and quantisation decisions are exact within declared integer limits. No Monte Carlo uncertainty or human data; no population generalisation. Fixed frame uses additional external timing information. Same original PCM renderer and matched digital peak bounds; actual signal energy can differ if pulses overlap. No actual listening, onset detection, clock drift or native browser qualification in this run.
