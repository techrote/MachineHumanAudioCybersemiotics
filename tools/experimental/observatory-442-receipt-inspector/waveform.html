Method and limits
Positive correlation with the known tapered triangular pulse is sampled every four samples, local peaks are refined on the sample grid, and descending-score nonmaximum suppression enforces 20 ms separation. Threshold is expressed relative to clean template autocorrelation, not a calibrated false-alarm probability. The decoder compares exactly three detected onsets to nominal candidate triples by L1 distance, abstaining on ties or count mismatch. The same decoder on supplied true indices isolates channel/model effects from detection errors. The clean-index comparator isolates quantisation ambiguity. No automatic clock recovery, unseen waveform, close overlapping pulses or human listener is tested. Gain/noise conditions are illustrative, not matched SNR or population estimates; timing MAE is conditional on exactly three detections and ordered pairing, not necessarily true association.


512 balanced trials, four fixed seeds paired across deletion labels, three predeclared thresholds. No fit or tuning; finite design summaries only.
