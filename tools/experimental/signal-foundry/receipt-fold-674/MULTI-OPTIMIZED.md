# FX-009M — Multi-pair receipt table optimization

Refs #674; #338/#339, `f8b9bddd89c2d174b28f060bf34b42d2ca9a0e64`, sections 2–4. Engineering optimization of the restricted finite model and creative audio interpretation. No human evidence or scientific acceptance.

The old table builder repeatedly encoded and decoded every state, recomputing finite-field products and preview scores. The new builder caches the base quantizer by resolution and unmasked GF products by resolution/multiplier. Linearity over XOR means a pair collides exactly when the masked product of its XOR difference is zero. Construct the cache from the products of basis vectors and their XOR combinations. Start from the base table, visit each pair once, and overwrite only the losing endpoint's bins. Known-action needs no edits. Compute the common preview digit only in preview mode. No changes to geometry, draw traversal, tie direction, ramp arithmetic, MR01, RF04, IDs or VST3 adapter. Same bounded audio-owner contract; no allocation, I/O or locks added.
